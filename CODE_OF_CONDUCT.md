# Code of Conduct

RunAny aims to be a welcoming project for contributors of all experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Spam or repeated off-topic comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that harms the project or community.

If you see a problem, contact the maintainer privately with relevant context and links.
