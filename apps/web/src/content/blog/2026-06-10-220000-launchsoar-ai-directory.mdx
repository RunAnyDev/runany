## What Is LaunchSoar?

LaunchSoar is a community-curated directory of AI-powered tools targeted at indie makers, creators, and startup founders. Instead of spending hours Googling for the right tool, you browse a focused catalog organized by category — video, audio, social media, productivity, and more.

The directory surfaces tools that are actually launching and gaining traction, not just tools that have existed for years. For anyone building in public or trying to ship fast, this kind of curated discovery saves real time.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Browse the Directory

Visit [https://launchsoar.com](https://launchsoar.com). The homepage shows featured and newest tools. Use the category filters on the left sidebar to narrow down by type — video, audio, social, writing, coding, etc.

### Step 2: Submit a Tool

If you have a tool you want to showcase:

1. Click **Submit a Tool** in the top navigation
2. Fill in the tool name, URL, category, and a short description
3. Submit for review

The community and moderators review submissions, so quality is generally decent.

### Step 3: Save and Share

Bookmark tools you want to try later. Share specific tool pages directly — each tool has its own public URL.

## Deeper Analysis

### What Makes It Different

Most AI tool directories are sprawling marketplaces with thousands of tools and no curation. LaunchSoar leans toward quality over quantity. Tools are submitted and reviewed, giving it a more handpicked feel compared to generic aggregator sites.

The HN launch gained solid early traction, which suggests the directory fills a real gap for the indie maker community.
