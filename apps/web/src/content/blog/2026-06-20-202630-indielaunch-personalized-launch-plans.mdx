## What Is Indie Launch?

Indie Launch is a web-based tool that helps indie developers and solo builders figure out where and how to launch their product. Instead of the typical "post everywhere" approach, it analyzes your product type, target audience, and existing presence to recommend 2–3 primary channels — then generates ready-made copy for each.

The core claim: most indie products fail not because of the product, but because the founder posted on the wrong platform. The site cites data from analyzing 387 indie launches, finding that Indie Hackers converts 23× better than Product Hunt for developer tools with no pre-existing audience.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Answer 4 Questions

The onboarding asks about:

- What your product does (one sentence)
- Who your primary audience is (developers, creators, or founders)
- Whether you have an existing audience on any platform
- Your current audience size on each platform

### Step 2: Get Your Personalized Plan

Within seconds, Indie Launch outputs a ranked channel list. Each channel gets a priority label (Primary or Secondary) and a plain-English rationale. For example, a developer tool with no existing audience and 200 Twitter followers might get:

1. **Hacker News** (Primary) — "Show HN, high intent, no audience needed"
2. **Reddit r/SideProject** (Secondary) — "Self-promo friendly, fast first feedback"
3. **Indie Hackers** (Secondary) — "23× better conversion than Product Hunt"

The platform matching is based on product type and where similar launches performed best in their dataset.

### Step 3: Ready-Made Content

For each recommended channel, you get:

- A pre-written post ready to copy and paste
- Platform-specific best practices (e.g., "Post Tuesday–Thursday between 8–10 AM EST" for Hacker News)
- What to avoid (e.g., "don't sound like marketing copy — HN rewards honest, technical posts from real builders")

The content adapts to your product description, not generic templates.

## Key Features

**Ready-made copy** — for 15+ platforms including Hacker News, Twitter/X, Reddit, Indie Hackers, LinkedIn, Product Hunt, and Dev.to. Copy button for each post.

**Up to 5 projects** — one payment covers multiple projects per account.

**One-time pricing** — $10, no subscription. Pay once, use forever.

## What It Does Not Do

Indie Launch generates the strategy and content — it does not post for you. You still need to:

- Create accounts on recommended platforms if you do not have them
- Engage with comments after posting
- Follow up on feedback loops

It is a launch planning tool, not an automated posting or growth service.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this useful if I already have a large following?**
**A:** Partially. The content generation is still useful, but the channel prioritization is most valuable for builders with little to no existing audience — where posting on the wrong platform wastes the most time.

**Q: Does it work for non-developer products?**
**A:** The site is clearly optimized for developer tools and indie SaaS. The channel data (387 launches) skews toward technical products. For consumer apps or e-commerce, the recommendations may be less precise.

**Q: Can I update my plan after answering?**
**A:** Yes. The dashboard lets you update your product description and regenerate the plan. Each project can be edited independently.

**Q: What happens if a platform changes its rules (e.g., Reddit's self-promo limits)?**
**A:** The site does not appear to auto-update recommendations for platform rule changes. Use the timing guides and self-promo rules as a baseline, but verify current subreddit rules before posting.
