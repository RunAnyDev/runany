## What Is Frontlyapp?

Frontlyapp is a no-code platform that uses AI to generate shareable apps directly from Google Sheets data. You describe what you want in plain language, and the platform builds an interface on top of your existing spreadsheet—no SQL, no backend, no code.

The platform positions itself as a bridge between Google Sheets and full custom software, targeting teams that need internal tools, client portals, or simple CRUD apps without a development cycle.

**Key capabilities:**

- AI-powered app generation from natural language prompts
- Connects to Google Sheets as a live data source
- Pre-built templates: Client Login Portal, Events Management, Expense Tracker, Daily Habit Tracker, Real Estate Listings
- Custom branding on paid plans
- API access on Professional tier

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Connect Your Google Account

Visit [frontlyapp.com](https://www.frontlyapp.com) and sign up with a Google account. Frontlyapp requests read/write access to your Google Drive to sync with Sheets.

### Step 2: Create or Connect a Google Sheet

Create a new Google Sheet or select an existing one. The sheet acts as your database—each tab becomes a data table in the generated app.

### Step 3: Describe Your App with AI

Use the AI prompt to describe the app you want. For example:

```
"Build a client portal where each client sees only their own
 submitted tickets, with a status dropdown and notes field."
```

Frontlyapp generates a working UI on top of your Sheet columns.

### Step 4: Customize and Publish

Adjust layouts, add or remove fields, and publish when ready. The app gets a shareable URL. No hosting configuration needed.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Frontlyapp work with existing Google Sheets or only new ones?**
**A:** Both. You can connect an existing Sheet or start from a template.

**Q: Can users edit data in the generated app?**
**A:** Yes, depending on how the app is configured. Editors can update Sheet rows through the generated UI.

**Q: What happens when operations credits run out?**
**A:** Paid plans specify monthly operation limits. Exceeding the limit on Basic ($30/mo) means 10,000 actions per month cap; overages require upgrading or waiting for reset.

**Q: Is there an API?**
**A:** API access is available on the Professional plan ($100/mo) only.
