## What Is Cardboard?

Cardboard is a Y Combinator W26-backed agentic video editor that runs entirely in the browser. Instead of manually trimming clips, adding captions, or repositioning framing, you type what you want – "Cut a 60-second brand film from the VO and site b-roll" – and Cardboard's AI director assembles it.

Key capabilities:

- **Natural language editing** – describe the edit in plain text and the AI executes it
- **Automatic cutting** – finds the best moments from raw footage and assembles a cut
- **Caption generation** – adds closed captions automatically
- **Reframe and compose** – AI reframes shots for different aspect ratios
- **No installs** – fully browser-based, works on any platform

## Setup Workflow

Cardboard requires no installation. Getting started takes under a minute.

### Step 1: Open the Editor

Navigate to [https://www.usecardboard.com](https://www.usecardboard.com) and sign up for an account. The editor opens in the browser with no plugins required.

### Step 2: Import Media

Drag and drop video files directly into the editor. Cardboard accepts common formats including MP4, MOV and WebM. Raw footage from any device works.

### Step 3: Describe Your Edit

Type a natural language prompt describing what you want. Examples:

```text
Cut a 60s brand film from the VO and the site b-roll
Create a 30s cutdown for LinkedIn
Add captions and reframe for vertical format
Assemble the product demo from the b-roll
```

### Step 4: Review and Export

Cardboard processes the footage, shows you the result, and asks if you want a revision. When satisfied, export in your desired resolution.

## Deeper Analysis

### How the AI Director Works

Cardboard's AI watches all imported clips, identifies the best moments based on visual and audio signals, then assembles the edit matching your prompt. It handles cutting, layering audio voice-over, caption placement and reframing in a single pass.

### Strengths

- **Speed** – a60-second brand film assembles in minutes from raw b-roll
- **Zero friction** – no software install, no export to external tools
- **Multi-format output** – can reframe one edit for horizontal, vertical and square
- **Caption automation** – saves the manual captioning step entirely

### Limitations

- **Requires clear prompts** – vague requests produce less useful results
- **Processing time** – longer footage takes more time to analyze
- **Internet required** – fully cloud-based, no offline mode

## Practical Evaluation Checklist

- Import media and describe an edit in under 2 minutes
- Verify captions are readable and properly timed
- Check reframed shots maintain visual quality
- Export at target resolution and verify in a video player
- Try a follow-up revision request ("make it 30 seconds shorter")

## Security Notes

- All processing happens on Cardboard's cloud infrastructure
- Media files are uploaded for processing; review the privacy policy for retention terms
- Browser sandboxing provides standard web security boundaries

## FAQ

**Q: Is Cardboard free to use?**
**A:** Cardboard offers a free tier with limited exports. Paid plans unlock higher resolution exports and longer footage.

**Q: What video formats does it support?**
**A:** MP4, MOV and WebM are confirmed. The editor handles most standard formats from cameras and phones.

**Q: Can I edit the AI's output?**
**A:** Yes. Cardboard shows you the assembled edit and lets you request revisions or make manual adjustments before exporting.

**Q: Does it work on mobile?**
**A:** The web editor works in mobile browsers, though a desktop browser provides a better editing experience.

**Q: How does it compare to Premiere Pro's AI features?**
**A:** Premiere's AI features (like Scene Edit Detection) assist manual editing. Cardboard takes a fully agentic approach – you describe the goal and it builds the edit without manual trim operations.
