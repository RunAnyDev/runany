## What Is Seede AI?

Seede AI is a web-based AI design tool targeted at non-designers who need marketing visuals quickly. You enter a text prompt describing what you want — a product announcement, a social media quote, an event poster — and the AI generates a complete graphic ready to download or share.

The pitch is straightforward: ideas into designs in 1 click. No Figma, no Canva expertise, no hiring a designer.

## Setup Workflow

Seede AI runs entirely in the browser. There is no installation.

### Step 1: Sign Up

Visit [seede.ai](https://seede.ai) and create a free account. The site supports email signup; social login options may be available depending on current configuration.

### Step 2: Enter Your Prompt

Once logged in, you are presented with a prompt input. Describe the poster you want as clearly as possible. Examples from the product page:

- "A minimalist product announcement poster for our new AI tool"
- "A bold social media post announcing our summer sale"
- "An event poster for a tech meetup next month"

### Step 3: Generate and Download

The AI generates your poster in seconds. You can download it as an image file and use it directly on social media, email campaigns, or print.

## Deeper Analysis

Seede AI occupies a specific niche: it targets the long tail of small business owners, indie makers, and marketers who need occasional marketing visuals but lack design skills or budget for a full-time designer.

The AI-generates-complete-graphic approach is different from tools like Canva (which provides templates you customize) or Midjourney (which generates images you then place in a design). Seede compresses the workflow to a single step.
