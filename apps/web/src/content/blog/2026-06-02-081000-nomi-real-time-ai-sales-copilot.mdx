## What Is Nomi?

Most founders aren't trained sales people. In the early days, you end up on live calls learning by trial and error — and one bad call can cost you a deal that would've closed if you'd just known the right phrase.

Nomi is a real-time AI that joins your sales calls via video and serves phrase suggestions exactly when it matters most:

- When a prospect pushes back — what to say to address the objection without sounding defensive
- When there's a hidden buying signal — what to dig into
- When it's time to close — and how to do it without sounding pushy

After the call, Nomi auto-generates clean CRM notes, action items, and sends a short email breakdown of what went well, what didn't, and how to improve — based on your actual conversation, not a generic template.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Join the Call

Nomi connects to your video call (Zoom, Meet, etc.) as a participant. You don't install anything special — it's a standard video call where Nomi is silently listening and analyzing in real time.

### Step 2: Real-Time Phrase Suggestions

Nomi's thinking model tracks the call's momentum, a tactic selector trained with reinforcement learning picks the right approach, and a lightweight LLM boosted with RAG delivers custom phrase suggestions in under 500ms.

The speed matters. If advice shows up 2 seconds late or is off-topic, it's worse than useless — it breaks your flow. The sub-500ms delivery is what makes this usable in a real conversation.

### Step 3: Post-Call Summary

Nomi auto-generates:

- Clean CRM-ready notes
- Action items from the call
- A coaching email breaking down what went well, what didn't, and how to improve next time

### Step 4: Copilot Learns From Your Calls

Each user gets a private copilot trained on their own calls (with permission), simulated data, and sales best practices. It gets sharper with every interaction — no manual tuning needed.

## Deeper Analysis

### What Makes Nomi Different

The real-time constraint is the hard part. Most AI sales tools are about coaching after the fact — you upload a call recording and get a transcript with timestamps. That's useful for learning, but it's not helping you in the moment.

Nomi's architecture is built around sub-500ms delivery from voice input to phrase suggestion. The stack includes:

- **Thinking Model** — tracks call momentum and context
- **Tactic Selector** — reinforcement learning trained on sales call patterns
- **Lightweight LLM with RAG** — delivers contextual suggestions fast

The product also learns from your actual calls, not just generic sales playbooks. Over time the suggestions become more personalized to how you sell and what your buyers respond to.
