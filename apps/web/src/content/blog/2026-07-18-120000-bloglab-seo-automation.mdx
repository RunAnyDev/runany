## What Is BlogLab?

BlogLab is a web-based AI platform built for SaaS founders and content marketers who want to automate blog production without sacrificing search visibility.

The core proposition is straightforward: connect your product, specify target keywords, and BlogLab generates drafts optimized for those terms. Rather than starting from a blank page, you get a structured post you can review and publish.

**Key claimed capabilities (from product page):**

- AI-generated blog posts targeting specific keywords
- SEO scoring per draft before publishing
- Automated internal linking suggestions
- CMS integration for direct publishing
- Bulk generation for content calendars

## Setup Workflow

BlogLab is a fully hosted web application — no installation required.

### Step 1: Sign Up

Visit [bloglab.app](https://bloglab.app) and create an account. The onboarding flow prompts you to add your SaaS product name and website URL.

### Step 2: Add Target Keywords

Input the keywords you want to rank for. BlogLab analyzes search volume and competition difficulty to prioritize which terms to target first.

### Step 3: Generate a Draft

Click **Generate** and the AI produces a full blog post around your keyword. You can regenerate with different angles or regenerate specific sections.

### Step 4: Review and Publish

Each draft comes with an SEO score. Edit in the built-in editor or export to your CMS (WordPress, Webflow, Ghost, etc.).
