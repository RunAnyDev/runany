## What Is AI Visibility Pro?

AI Visibility Pro is a SEO-focused tool for the AI era. Rather than optimizing for Google search alone, it audits how well AI systems — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Grok — can crawl, understand, verify, and cite your brand's web presence.

The core product is a **GEO Panorama Audit**: a full report that maps crawlability, brand entity clarity, evidence signals, intent coverage, technical structure, trust signals, and competitor gaps. It translates those findings into a prioritized fix roadmap (P0/P1/P2) that teams can hand directly to engineers and content writers.

## Deeper Analysis

### What the GEO Audit Covers

From the product page, the full audit includes checks for:

- **Full-site crawl inventory** — homepage, product, pricing, case studies, docs, help center, blog, sitemap, robots, PDFs, and hidden entry points
- **Brand entity profile** — who you are, what you offer, product boundaries, aliases, target customers, use cases, regions, and disambiguation risks
- **Evidence and trust signals** — whether your site gives AI systems enough verifiable facts to cite
- **Intent coverage** — whether your content covers the queries AI systems use to find businesses like yours
- **Competitor gaps** — what competitors have that you do not, in AI-visible terms

### Pricing Model

The tool uses a **free-to-lock, pay-to-unlock** model:

- **Free**: locked report showing structure, summary, and top-level signals
- **Credits**: purchased as packs, valid for 1 year, deducted when unlocking a generated report

The credit-based model means you only pay for what you use, and the 1-year validity gives teams flexibility in how they pace their audits.

## Practical Evaluation Checklist

Use this checklist when running your own GEO audit:

- [ ] Submit your root domain (not just homepage) for the broadest crawl
- [ ] Review the brand entity section — does it correctly identify your products and target customers?
- [ ] Check the P0 items first — these are technical blockers that prevent AI systems from crawling content
- [ ] Cross-reference the competitor gap section against known competitors in your space

- [ ] Treat the 90-day publishing roadmap as a content queue, not a checklist — prioritize intent coverage gaps first

## Security Notes

AI Visibility Pro operates as a read-only crawler. It fetches and analyzes your publicly accessible web pages — it does not require login credentials, API keys, or authenticated access to your site.

As with any external audit tool:

- Do not submit internal staging URLs or URLs behind authentication
- Review the tool's data retention and privacy policy before auditing a client site
- The audit report contains sensitive findings about your site's AI-visible gaps — treat it as internal-only

## FAQ

**Q: Does the audit guarantee my brand will be cited by ChatGPT or Claude?**
**A:** No. The audit improves readiness by making your website easier for AI systems to crawl, understand, verify, and cite. AI platforms make their own ranking and citation decisions independently.

**Q: What is the difference between GEO and traditional SEO?**
**A:** Traditional SEO optimizes for Google search rankings. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes for AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and similar — which synthesize information differently than search engines do. GEO focuses on entity clarity, evidence signals, and structured data that AI systems can verify.

**Q: Do I need technical knowledge to use the report?**
**A:** The free locked report is readable by anyone on the marketing or growth team. The full unlocked report includes code-level schema suggestions that will need a developer to implement. The P0/P1/P2 priority format is designed to be actionable without deep technical SEO expertise.

**Q: How long does an audit take?**
**A:** The free locked report generates quickly from a URL submission. The time to unlock and receive a full report depends on site size and the credit purchase flow.

**Q: Can I audit client websites with this tool?**
**A:** Yes — agencies and consultants are listed as a target user. You would purchase credits and run audits on behalf of clients.
