## TL;DR

> **TL;DR:** Looties is a marketplace for buying, selling, and discovering tech swag — hoodies, stickers, conference merch, and onboarding kits — with AI-native listings from the companies you follow.

## What Is Looties?

[Looties](https://looties.io/) is a community marketplace where developers, indie hackers, and tech companies can buy, sell, and discover rare tech swag. Think of it as eBay for the tech community — but focused specifically on the kind of gear that ends up in your closet from hackathons, conferences, and indie launches.

The platform covers:

- **Hoodies and apparel** from indie tools and YC companies
- **Stickers and patches** for laptops and water bottles
- **Conference merch** from past events you missed
- **Onboarding kits** sold directly by the companies themselves
- **Rare and limited drops** from popular developer tools

> **From the product:** "Buy, sell, and discover rare tech swag: hoodies, stickers, conference merch, and onboarding kits. AI-native listings from the tech companies you love."

The "AI-native" angle means sellers can create listings programmatically — companies can list their official merch at scale, and collectors can track down specific company gear more easily than hunting through generic marketplaces.

## Key Features

### Buy Tech Swag Directly from Companies

Instead of hunting for that hoodie from a startup you follow, Looties lets companies list their own merch directly. If a tool ships an onboarding kit or branded gear, it goes here — alongside the community resellers.

### Sell Your Conference Haul

Attended a conference and grabbed too much swag? List it on Looties. The platform handles the listing flow for individual sellers who want to clear out their closet without dealing with generic classifieds.

### AI-Native Listings

The "AI-native" descriptor in the product description suggests listings are created or enriched programmatically. For companies, this likely means a faster bulk-listing process rather than manual item-by-item creation.

### Discovery for Collectors

The marketplace structure surfaces rare and limited drops. If you missed a specific company's launch merch, it may show up in searches or recommendations rather than being buried in a generic marketplace.

## How Looties Works

1. **Browse or search** for a company, product, or item type
