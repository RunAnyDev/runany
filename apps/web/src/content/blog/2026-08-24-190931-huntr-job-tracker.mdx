## What Is Huntr?

Huntr is a cloud-based job search management platform that combines application tracking, AI-powered resume building, and automated cover letter generation into a single workspace. The core differentiator is its **semantic matching** engine — it analyzes both your resume and target job description to identify keyword gaps and alignment score before you apply.

Unlike a basic spreadsheet tracker, Huntr treats each job as a document pair (resume + job posting) and surfaces how well they match before you hit submit.

The platform is entirely free for job seekers. Revenue appears to come from optional premium features rather than charging applicants.

### Core Features

- **Job tracker** — organize applications by status, company, role, salary range, and notes
- **AI resume builder** — paste a job description, Huntr rewrites your resume bullets to match the language and requirements
- **Semantic match score** — quantifies how closely your resume aligns with a given job posting
- **Cover letter generator** — produces tailored letters per application using the job description as context
- **Application autofill** — one-click form filling using stored profile data
- **Free tier** — no signup cost or trial limit for core job tracking features

## Setup Workflow

Huntr runs entirely in the browser with no installation required.

### Step 1: Create an Account

Navigate to [huntr.co](https://huntr.co) and click **Sign up for free**. You can sign up with an email address or continue with Google OAuth.

### Step 2: Import or Build Your Base Resume

From the dashboard, go to **Resume** and either:
- Upload an existing resume file (PDF or DOCX), or
- Start from scratch using Huntr's guided form

The resume is stored as a templated profile — not a static file — so it can be dynamically rewritten per job application.

### Step 3: Connect a Job Posting

Click **Add Job** and paste a job description URL or raw text. Huntr parses the posting and extracts:
- Required skills and keywords
- Role title and company
- Preferred qualifications

### Step 4: Generate a Matched Resume

With a job open, click **Tailor Resume**. Huntr's AI rewrites your base resume bullets to mirror the language, keyword density, and requirement ordering of the target role.

A match score (0–100) appears before you export. Re-edit until the score is in your target range.

### Step 5: Generate a Cover Letter

Click **Cover Letter** on the same job card. Huntr generates a letter using your profile data and the job description as context. You can edit the output before copying or downloading.

## Deeper Analysis

### Semantic Matching Explained

Most resume tailoring tools swap keywords naively (insert "Python" if the posting mentions Python). Huntr goes a layer deeper by analyzing **semantic context** — understanding that "led a team of 5 engineers" and "managed a small engineering squad" are equivalent experiences even if the exact words differ.

This matters for two reasons:
1. Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) often score based on semantic similarity, not just keyword frequency
2. Recruiters spend under 10 seconds on a resume — matching their mental model of the ideal candidate reduces friction

The match score is directional, not authoritative. Treat it as a heuristic for whether your resume tells the same story as the job posting, not a pass/fail gate.

### Privacy Considerations

Huntr stores your resume content, job applications, and career history on their servers. Before uploading, consider:

- **What personal data is in your resume?** — home address, phone number, personal email are common. Decide whether you want those fields in Huntr's database.
- **Company visibility** — Huntr's free tier means your activity data may inform product improvements. Review their privacy policy for details on data retention and third-party sharing.
- **Password and OAuth** — if using Google OAuth, Huntr receives your Google profile (name, email). If using email/password, ensure you use a strong, unique password.

## Security Notes

- Huntr uses HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
- Resume content is stored in Huntr's database (not locally on your device in the free tier)
- No two-factor authentication (2FA) visible on the free plan — consider this if your career data is sensitive
- Carefully review what personal information appears in your uploaded resume before adding it to the platform

## FAQ

**Q: Is Huntr really free?**
**A:** Yes, the core job tracking, AI resume tailoring, and cover letter generation are free. The pricing page mentions premium tiers for advanced features, but the base plan has no stated time limit.

**Q: Does Huntr work for all job sites?**
**A:** Huntr works best with job descriptions you paste manually or from well-structured job boards. Sites that render descriptions entirely in JavaScript may require copying the text manually.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
**A:** Yes, you can download your resume and job application data. Always export and back up your job list — platform lock-in means losing access if the service changes or shuts down.

**Q: Does Huntr guarantee I will get hired?**
**A:** No. Huntr is a workflow tool that improves resume relevance and organization. Match scores do not influence whether a human recruiter advances your application.

Huntr addresses a real pain point in the job search process — the tedious, error-prone work of tailoring one resume for dozens of different roles. The free tier makes it accessible without commitment. Try connecting a job you have already applied to and running the match score — it takes under two minutes and gives an immediate read on how well your current resume tells the right story.

If you are actively job searching and tired of copying/pasting into every application, Huntr is worth a look.
