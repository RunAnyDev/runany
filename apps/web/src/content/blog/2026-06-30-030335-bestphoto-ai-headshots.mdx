## What Is BestPhoto AI?

BestPhoto is an AI-powered photo generation platform that creates professional-quality headshots, portraits, and social media content from existing photos you already have. Rather than booking a photographer or studio session, you upload 10-15 photos of yourself and the AI generates dozens of high-quality variations — headshots for LinkedIn, dating profile pictures, professional portraits for business websites, and more.

The platform is aimed at professionals, job seekers, freelancers, and anyone who needs quality photos without the cost and logistics of a traditional photo shoot. The process is entirely cloud-based and takes roughly 40 minutes to 1 hour from upload to delivery, with email notification when results are ready.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Upload Your Photos

After signing up (free, no credit card required), you upload 10-15 clear photos of yourself. The platform recommends:

- Good lighting (natural light works well)
- Different angles (front-facing and slight turns)
- Various expressions
- Different backgrounds
- Avoid heavy filters, sunglasses, or partially covered faces

### Step 2: AI Processing

The AI trains a personal model on your uploaded photos. This model is used to generate new images in various styles — professional studio headshots, creative portraits, themed content for specific platforms.

### Step 3: Download and Use

Once generation completes (typically under 1 hour), you receive a pack of high-resolution photos ready for download. Paid plans include full commercial usage rights, meaning you can use them on LinkedIn, business websites, marketing materials, and social media.
