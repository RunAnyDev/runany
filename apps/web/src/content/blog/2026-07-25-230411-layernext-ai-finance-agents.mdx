## What Is LayerNext?

LayerNext is a purpose-built AI agent platform for finance operations teams. Rather than a general-purpose chatbot or RAG tool, LayerNext agents are trained on the specific workflows of accounts payable (AP), bank reconciliation, bookkeeping close cycles, and CFO reporting.

The product emerged from the founders' own struggle as business operators — repeatedly manually chasing receipts, reconciling accounts, and closing books month after month. LayerNext agents handle these workflows automatically, integrating directly with the ERP and accounting systems a business already runs.

Key capabilities:
- **AP Automation** — agents process invoices, match POs, and route approvals without manual data entry
- **Bank Reconciliation** — automated matching of bank transactions against ledger entries, handling partial matches and edge cases
- **Bookkeeping Close** — monthly close cycle automation that keeps books "always closed" rather than playing catch-up
- **CFO Reporting** — structured financial reporting delivered on demand rather than assembled manually

LayerNext agents connect to QuickBooks, NetSuite, SAP, and other ERPs. They plan, execute, and self-verify results, and learn from every correction the finance team makes.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Connect Your ERP

LayerNext integrates with your existing accounting stack. Supported systems include:

- QuickBooks Online and Desktop
- NetSuite
- SAP
- Other major ERPs (check the integrations page for the full list)

You connect your ERP during the initial setup wizard. No coding required — select your ERP from a list and authorize the connection.

### Step 2: Choose Your Plan

LayerNext offers three tiers:

| Plan | Price | Transactions/mo | Insights/mo |
|------|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| Starter AI Bookkeeping | $79/month | 100 | 10 |
| Pro AI Bookkeeping + CFO Insights | $299/month | 500 | 20 |
| Enterprise Custom | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited |

- Starter: automated bank reconciliation, basic cashflow view, monthly summary report, priority email support
- Pro: full bookkeeping automation plus CFO-level financial visibility, real-time books
- Enterprise: custom scaling, multi-entity control, advanced automation, dedicated support

All plans include a 7-day free trial. Overage transactions beyond the plan limit are charged at $0.60/transaction (Starter) or $0.40/transaction (Pro).

### Step 3: Deploy Your First Agent

After connecting your ERP and selecting a plan, you configure your first agent:

1. Select the workflow type (AP, reconciliation, bookkeeping close, or CFO reporting)
2. Set transaction and insight limits per your plan
3. Configure approval routing if human-in-the-loop sign-offs are required
4. Activate — the agent begins processing immediately

Agents can also be configured with role-based permissions for your finance team members.

### Step 4: Review and Correct

LayerNext agents self-verify results and learn from corrections. The dashboard shows all processing activity, flagged items, and learned patterns. Your team reviews flagged items, makes corrections, and the agent incorporates those corrections into future runs.

## Deeper Analysis

### Target User

LayerNext is designed for:
- Small to mid-size businesses doing bookkeeping manually or with limited finance staff
- Finance teams spending more than 10 hours/month on reconciliation and close
- CFOs who want real-time financial visibility without waiting for monthly reports
- Companies running QuickBooks, NetSuite, or SAP who want automation without ripping out their existing stack

### Strengths

- **Deep ERP integration** — connects natively to QuickBooks, NetSuite, and SAP rather than relying on screen scraping or CSV imports
- **Self-verifying agents** — agents check their own work before surfacing results, reducing the review burden
- **Learning from corrections** — each human correction improves future agent performance
- **Real-time books** — unlike traditional monthly close cycles, LayerNext keeps books current continuously
- **No-code deployment** — finance team can configure and deploy agents without developer involvement

### Limitations

- No free tier beyond the 7-day trial — ongoing use requires a paid subscription
- Transaction-based pricing can become expensive for high-volume AP departments
- No open-source option — proprietary SaaS only
- Enterprise pricing is only available via sales contact, making cost comparison difficult

### Pricing Context

At $79/month for 100 transactions and 10 insights, LayerNext is competitively positioned against:
- Manual bookkeeping staff or fractional CFOs
- Traditional bookkeeping software that still requires human operation
- Enterprise CFO tools that require large internal finance teams to operate

The 7-day free trial lets teams validate the product against their actual transaction volume before committing.
