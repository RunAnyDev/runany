## What Is Rataify?

Rataify is a website trust and compliance scanner built for agencies, freelancers, small business owners, and SaaS founders. You add a URL, run a scan, and receive a prioritized action plan covering:

- **WCAG accessibility** — flags barriers that affect disabled users
- **GDPR/CCPA privacy** — surfaces cookie consent, data collection, and privacy policy gaps
- **FTC marketing risk** — checks for potentially deceptive claims or misleading content
- **SEO credibility** — evaluates signals that affect search visibility and trust
- **Conversion blockers** — identifies UX and trust elements that hurt turn rates

The core workflow is: add website, run scan, review score, fix issues, rescan, share the client report. Rataify generates client-ready summaries so agencies can explain findings to stakeholders without legal jargon.

> **Important:** Rataify is not a legal certification service. It surfaces compliance-related signals to help teams know what to review and improve — it does not replace legal counsel.

## How Rataify Works

### Step 1: Enter a URL

Navigate to [rataify.com](https://www.rataify.com) and enter the website URL you want to audit. No account required for an initial scan.

### Step 2: Run the Scan

Rataify crawls the target site and analyzes it across five compliance dimensions simultaneously. Scans typically complete within a few minutes depending on site size.

### Step 3: Review the Action Plan

The dashboard presents findings ranked by severity. Each issue includes:

- A plain-language explanation of the problem
- The compliance area it affects (accessibility, privacy, marketing, etc.)
- A suggested fix
- A rescan button to verify improvements

### Step 4: Share Client Reports

Rataify formats findings into client-ready summaries. Agencies can use these to justify fix priorities to clients and demonstrate progress after remediation.

## Security Notes

Rataify scans publicly accessible web pages only. No authentication credentials or private data are required for a standard trust scan. As with any third-party scanning service, review their privacy policy before scanning sensitive or internal-facing URLs.
