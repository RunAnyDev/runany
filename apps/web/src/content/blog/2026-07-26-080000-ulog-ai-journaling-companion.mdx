## TL;DR

> **TL;DR:** uLog.ai is a conversational AI journaling tool that asks you questions each day and builds a searchable timeline of your life — from $2/month.

## What Is uLog.ai?

uLog.ai is a web-based journaling tool that replaces blank-page anxiety with an AI conversation. Instead of staring at an empty text area, you pick or create a topic, set optional reminders, and receive adaptive questions generated by ChatGPT. Your responses are stored as a searchable, editable timeline — something between a diary and a personal knowledge base.

The pitch on [the HN launch thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=36126485):

> "The inundation of information in our digital age often makes it challenging to remember what truly matters. uLog.ai is a solution to this problem, aiming to simplify this process by creating a dynamic, engaging, and personal experience."

## How It Works

The workflow is straightforward and deliberate:

1. **Create or pick a topic** — a broad theme for your journaling session (e.g., "work reflection", "health tracking", "creative ideas")
2. **Set optional reminders** — schedule daily or weekly prompts so journaling becomes a habit
3. **AI asks adaptive questions** — the system generates follow-up questions based on what you wrote in previous sessions
4. **Summaries build a timeline** — the AI surfaces patterns and connections over time, creating a readable timeline view
5. **Edit and reflect anytime** — all entries are fully editable after the fact

- **Privacy** — No GitHub repo means the codebase is not publicly auditable. If self-hosting is a requirement, this is not a fit.
- **Timeline view** — Being able to scroll a chronological summary of past sessions is the feature that separates uLog from a standard notes app.
- **Reminders** — Optional push or email reminders help establish a journaling habit, a common failure point for journaling apps.
- **Data ownership** — Export capabilities were not confirmed from the landing page.
