## What Is Markey?

Markey is a web-based launch platform built for indie hackers and solo founders who want a full launch week without writing every post by hand. You give it your product URL — Markey reads the page and generates platform-native content for X, LinkedIn, Reddit, blog, email, and other channels.

The key differentiator is the **weekly drip**: after launch week, Markey continues dropping fresh posts on a schedule so your product stays in feeds rather than disappearing after a single launch day. It also includes a **Signal Radar** that scans Reddit and X for conversations relevant to your product, and a **Reply Radar** that drafts on-brand responses to warm inbound mentions.

Markey targets builders using vibe-coding tools: Bolt, Lovable, v0, Cursor, and Claude Code. The workflow assumes you have a deployed URL ready — not a GitHub repo or a marketing brief, just the product itself.

## Key Features

### Launch Pack Generator
Markey generates 30+ content types from a single URL:
- X tweet and X thread
- Threads, Bluesky, and LinkedIn posts
- Reddit post
- Instagram and TikTok captions
- Blog post copy
- Launch email and newsletter drop
- Product Hunt launch submission
- Hacker News post and Indie Hackers post
- Press release and cold email outreach templates

You can publish directly or copy-paste. All content is drafted in your brand voice.

### Signal Radar
Scans Reddit threads and X posts for conversations matching your product and audience. Signals are scored by intent, recency, and fit so you can find warm inbound conversations to join without manual searching.

### Reply Radar
Drafts on-brand replies to relevant inbound mentions. You review and paste — designed to convert warm inbound into actual users without the manual back-and-forth.

### Weekly Content Drip
After launch week, Markey rotates fresh posts on a configurable schedule so you stay in feeds. Last week's content mix is preserved as a rotating carousel rather than going silent.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Sign Up

Visit [markey.app](https://markey.app) and click **Start free trial**. Choose the Hobby or Builder plan after your trial ends.

### Step 2: Connect Your Product URL

Paste your deployed product URL into Markey's campaign input. Markey reads the page and extracts the key value propositions automatically.

### Step 3: Select Your Channels

Pick the platforms that matter for your launch. You do not need to use all 30+ content types — select X, LinkedIn, and Reddit if that is your core audience.

### Step 4: Generate and Publish

Markey generates platform-native posts. You can auto-publish or copy-paste manually. The same workflow applies to the weekly drip after launch week.

## Practical Evaluation Checklist

- **What it does well:** Automates the tedious multi-channel launch copy that most indie hackers hand-write or skip entirely. Signal and Reply Radars address the discovery gap that most launch guides skip.
- **What it does not do:** It generates first-draft copy, not final copy. You still need to review and edit for accuracy, tone, and any product details Markey could not infer from the URL.
- **Not a substitute for:** Building an actual audience before launch. If you have zero traction channels, Markey makes the content — but someone still needs to be reading it.
- **Best for:** Indie builders with a deployed product and at least one active social presence who want to run a structured launch without hiring a copywriter or spending a week writing posts.
- **Open source:** No. Markey is a hosted SaaS with no self-hosted or open-source option.

## Security Notes

Markey reads your product URL to generate content. No authentication tokens or API keys are required from your side. Review the [security page](https://markey.app/security) before connecting a URL pointing to an internal staging environment.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Markey work with GitHub Pages or Netlify deploys?**
**A:** Yes — any publicly accessible URL works. It reads the deployed page, not the source repo.

**Q: Can I use Markey without connecting my social accounts?**
**A:** Yes. You can copy generated copy and paste it manually. Auto-publish requires OAuth connections to each platform.

**Q: Does Markey post on my behalf automatically?**
**A:** Only if you enable auto-publish per channel. The default is generate-and-copy so you review everything before it goes live.

**Q: Is there a free tier beyond the trial?**
**A:** No permanent free tier — the 7-day trial gives full Hobby access. Pricing starts at $12/mo after the trial.

## Conclusion

Markey fills a specific gap in the indie hacker launch stack: the multi-channel content grunt work that sits between "product is ready" and "launch is live." Its weekly drip feature is the most differentiated part — most launch guides focus on day-one spikes, not the multi-week visibility that compounds over time.

If you are tired of writing 15 variations of your launch post across platforms, or if your launch goes silent after a single HN submission, Markey is worth a 7-day trial. It is not a replacement for genuine audience building, but it removes one of the most common execution gaps that separates a quiet launch from a visible one.
