## What Is Shimmer?

Shimmer is an ADHD coaching platform designed specifically for adults. Unlike generic productivity apps, it is built around how ADHD brains actually work — not around idealized workflows that assume consistent focus.

The core offering:

- **1:1 Video Coaching** — Get matched with a dedicated ADHD-trained coach for weekly sessions. Coaches help with goal-setting, accountability, and strategy customization.
- **Body Doubling** — On-demand sessions where a coach or community member sits with you virtually while you work. This leverages the ADHD strategy of external accountability and reduced task isolation.
- **Community Access** — Peer community with thousands of adults with ADHD sharing strategies, wins, and setbacks in a judgment-free space.
- **Learning Journeys** — Science-backed modules covering executive function, emotional regulation, work performance, and more.
- **Weekly Workshops & Panels** — Live events with ADHD experts and guest speakers.

## Deeper Analysis

### What Sets Shimmer Apart

The biggest differentiator is the **body doubling** feature. Body doubling is a well-documented ADHD strategy where having another person present (physically or virtually) reduces the friction of starting and staying on task.

Most productivity apps assume you have the executive function to start tasks independently. Shimmer acknowledges that many adults with ADHD need an external structure to initiate and sustain effort. The on-demand body doubling is a direct response to this.

The coaching model is also distinct. Shimmer coaches are specifically ADHD-trained, not general life coaches. This matters because ADHD has unique neurological underpinnings that require tailored approaches — generic productivity advice often fails for people with ADHD.

### Pricing

Shimmer's pricing is structured around coaching tiers. Expect to pay for the coaching component plus platform access. The 30-day money-back guarantee reduces the risk of trying it.

### Recommended By Clinicians

## FAQ

**Q: Is Shimmer only for people with diagnosed ADHD?**
**A:** No. Shimmer is open to anyone who identifies with ADHD traits or struggles with focus, executive function, or task initiation — regardless of formal diagnosis.

**Q: How is body doubling different from just working in a coffee shop?**
**A:** Body doubling with Shimmer is structured. A coach or community member actively checks in, provides accountability, and can help you break through resistance. A coffee shop provides passive presence — Shimmer provides active, targeted support.

**Q: Can I use Shimmer without doing 1:1 coaching?**
**A:** Coaching is a core part of the Shimmer model. The platform is designed to pair community and learning with regular coaching sessions.

**Q: How is Shimmer different from therapy?**
**A:** Shimmer coaches are not therapists — they are ADHD coaching specialists. Therapy typically focuses on processing emotions and trauma. ADHD coaching focuses on practical strategies, systems, and accountability for daily functioning.
