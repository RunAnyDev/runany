## What Is AppLaunchFlow?

AppLaunchFlow is a web-based AI tool that automates the tedious process of creating App Store and Play Store screenshots and ASO (App Store Optimization) assets. Upload your app screenshots, and the AI generates multiple screenshot variants with localized text, device frames, and marketing copy already applied.

The core workflow:
1. Upload raw app screenshots (iPhone, Android)
2. Select target markets and languages
3. AI generates device-framed screenshots with marketing overlays
4. Export ready-to-upload assets for App Store Connect / Play Console

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Sign Up

Visit [applaunchflow.com](https://www.applaunchflow.com) and create a free account. No credit card required for the free tier.

### Step 2: Upload Screenshots

Drag and drop your app screenshots (PNG or JPG). AppLaunchFlow accepts screenshots at1x, 2x, and 3x resolution. Larger files upload faster with compression on the client side.

### Step 3: Choose Template and Language

Select from pre-built App Store and Play Store screenshot templates. Choose the languages and locales you want to target — the AI handles the translation and text overlay automatically.

### Step 4: Generate and Download

Click **Generate** and wait30–60 seconds per variant. Download individual screenshots or bulk-export as a ZIP archive ready for App Store Connect.

## Deeper Analysis

### What Makes It Different

Traditional ASO asset creation requires a designer to manually frame screenshots, write copy, and manage translations. AppLaunchFlow compresses this into a single AI-powered pipeline. The translation quality depends on the underlying model — for niche markets, a human review is still recommended.
