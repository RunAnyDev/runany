## What Is Steadwing?

Steadwing is an AI-powered autonomous on-call engineer. Instead of waking a human up at 2 AM to dig through logs and guess at root cause, Steadwing automatically:

- Correlates evidence across your entire stack
- Identifies the likely root cause within minutes of an alert firing
- Produces an actionable RCA (Root Cause Analysis) with specific remediation steps
- Lets you ask follow-up questions about the incident and your infrastructure

It positions itself as the "AI SRE" your team didn't know you needed. The pitch from the founders on HN: connect once, resolve incidents forever.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Connect Your Integrations

Steadwing plugs into your existing alerting pipeline. Supported integrations include:

- **Slack** — receive incident reports and interact with Steadwing via `/steadwing` commands
- **Sentry** — pull error events and stack traces as evidence
- **Elasticsearch** — query log indices for correlation data
- **Datadog**, **PagerDuty**, **Grafana** — additional alert sources (verify current list on the integrations page)

Connect each integration once via the dashboard at [app.steadwing.com](https://app.steadwing.com/signup). No credit card required to start. A demo mode is available if you want to explore before connecting real infrastructure.

### Step 2: Configure Alert Routing

Route specific alert types to Steadwing. High-severity alerts (P1/P2) trigger immediate investigation. Lower-severity alerts batch for periodic review. You control the routing rules from the policies panel.

### Step 3: Receive Incident Reports

When an alert fires, Steadwing begins correlating evidence automatically. Within 5 minutes you receive:

- **Timeline** — what happened, in chronological order
- **Root cause hypothesis** — the most likely culprit based on correlated signals
- **Remediation steps** — concrete actions to resolve the incident
- **Ask follow-up** — query Steadwing about specifics, e.g., "which deployment preceded this?"

## Security Notes

Steadwing requires read access to your alerting and log infrastructure (Sentry, Elasticsearch, etc.) to correlate evidence. It does not require write access to production systems by default. Evaluate whether the tool's evidence-gathering scope aligns with your security policy before connecting production environments.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Steadwing auto-apply fixes?**
**A:** It suggests remediation steps. Whether those get auto-applied depends on your configuration. The default is human-in-the-loop: Steadwing recommends, you approve.

**Q: What happens to my incident data?**
**A:** Steadwing processes incident data to generate RCA reports. Check the privacy policy at steadwing.com for data retention specifics.

**Q: Is there a self-hosted option?**
**A:** Not currently. Steadwing is a cloud-hosted service.
