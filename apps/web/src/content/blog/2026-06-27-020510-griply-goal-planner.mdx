## What Is Griply?

Griply is a goal planner that connects your vision, goals, habits, and tasks into one unified system. Built by a bootstrapped team, it started as an iOS app and pivoted to desktop after user feedback showed most real planning happens on desktop.

The core philosophy is simple: **everything starts with why**. Rather than just listing tasks, Griply structures work around life areas (health, career, relationships), connects them to a long-term vision, and breaks them down into measurable goals with daily habits and tasks.

The planning hierarchy in Griply looks like this:

```
life areas → vision → goals → habits/tasks → calendar/daily planner
```

Griply 3.0 (released around the July 2025 launch) added three major features:

1. **Goal Roadmap** — plan goals across months or years
2. **Time Blocking** — schedule time for goals and life areas, not just individual tasks
3. **Built-in Calendar** — with Google Calendar integration

It is available on Mac, Windows, and Web.

## Setup and First Run

Griply is a desktop-first application. To get started:

1. Visit [griply.app](https://griply.app) and navigate to the download section
2. Download the version for your OS (Mac, Windows, or use the web app)
3. Install and launch — no account required to explore the free tier
4. Create your first **life area** (e.g., Health, Career, Relationships)
5. Define your long-term **vision** within each life area
6. Break vision into **goals**, then into **habits** and **tasks**
7. Use the Daily Planner and Calendar to schedule time blocks

The product page at `griply.app/v3` includes a walkthrough video covering all the new 3.0 features.

## How It Differs From Todoist or Things 3

Todoist and Things 3 are task managers. They excel at capturing and organizing tasks, but they treat goals as an afterthought (projects in Todoist, areas in Things). Griply inverts this: goals come first, tasks exist to serve goals.

Key differences:

| Feature | Griply | Todoist | Things 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Life areas / areas | Native | Labels (workaround) | Native |
| Goal hierarchy | Core feature | None | Projects only |
| Time blocking | Built-in | No | No |
| Vision → Task flow | Direct link | None | None |
| Calendar | Built-in + Google Cal | No | No |
| Free tier | Yes | Yes (limited) | Trial only |

The HN discussion around the launch noted that many users switching from Things 3 or Todoist appreciated this structural difference — specifically that long-term goals stay visible alongside daily actions.

## Conclusion

Griply fills a specific gap in the productivity app space: tools like Todoist and Things 3 handle tasks well but don't connect them to a goal system. Griply makes that connection explicit — your daily habits and tasks are always visible in the context of a larger goal and life area.

The July 2025 launch of version 3.0 with Goal Roadmap, Time Blocking, and Calendar integration shows the product is actively evolving. The desktop-first pivot after user feedback suggests the team listens to real users.

If you have been bouncing between Notion for goals, a habit app for daily tracking, and Todoist for tasks — and find the disconnection frustrating — Griply is worth a look.
