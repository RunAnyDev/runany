## TL;DR

> **TL;DR:** CloddsBot is an open source AI trading agent that operates autonomously across multiple prediction-market and crypto exchanges — Polymarket, Kalshi, Binance, and Hyperliquid. The stack is TypeScript, the agent brain is Claude, and the goal is a single bot that watches every venue and acts when it spots a setup. It is not a no-loss arbitrage bot; it is an autonomous trader you can read and modify.

## What Is CloddsBot?

The repository description: "Open Source AI trading agent that operates autonomously across 1000+ markets - Polymarket, Kalshi, Binance, Hyperliquid." That is the entire pitch — a single bot that watches multiple venues and trades on them without per-exchange glue code.

The topics — `agi`, `ai`, `arbitrage`, `claude` — confirm that the bot uses Anthropic's Claude as the reasoning layer, and that arbitrage is at least one of the strategies it can run.

## How It Works

The TypeScript codebase wires up connectors to each venue and an agent loop on top:

- Market data feeds from Polymarket, Kalshi, Binance, and Hyperliquid are normalized into a single stream the agent can read.
- The agent, powered by Claude, decides whether to act on the current state.
- When it decides to trade, the same connectors place and manage orders on the right venue.
- The bot logs every decision, so the user can audit what the model did and why.

The "1000+ markets" claim comes from the breadth of those venues combined — each exchange has its own order book and market list, and the bot treats them as one addressable surface.

## When To Use It

CloddsBot is the right tool when:

- You want a single open source bot that spans prediction markets and crypto exchanges.
- You are comfortable reading and modifying TypeScript and operating it yourself.
- You accept that any autonomous trading system can lose money and treat this as a research surface, not a turnkey income source.

It is not a hosted signal service, not a guaranteed-returns product, and not a substitute for understanding the markets it touches. Trading rules and tax obligations still apply in your jurisdiction. Read the project's risk notes before turning it on with real funds.
