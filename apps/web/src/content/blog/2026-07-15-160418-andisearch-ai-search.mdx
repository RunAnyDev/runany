## What Is Andi?

Most search engines return a list of links and leave the answering work to you. Andi takes a different approach — it reads multiple sources and returns a direct answer to your question, formatted as a chat-like response with cited sources alongside.

Andi launched on Hacker News in [YC W22 (2022)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=30832589), where it reached 352 points. The team behind it describes it as "AI search for the next generation — instead of just links, Andi gives you answers like chatting with a smart friend."

### Key characteristics

- **Answer-first**: Andi generates a synthesized answer rather than a link index
- **Ad-free**: No advertising, no promoted results
- **Anti-spam**: Results filtered to reduce low-quality and AI-generated content
- **Academic search**: Early HN commenters noted particularly strong results for academic queries
- **Progressive Web App**: Loads as a PWA with background update behavior

## Security Notes

A [comment on the HN launch thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=30832589) flagged that Andi sends analytics to `analytics.andisearch.com` with UUID tracking. If privacy-first search is a hard requirement, verify the full data handling in the privacy policy before relying on it for sensitive queries.
