## What Is AI Visibility?

AI Visibility is a free tool that measures how often and how prominently AI assistants reference a brand when answering queries. Unlike traditional SEO tools that track Google rankings, AI Visibility shows whether your brand appears in responses from ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google), and Perplexity.

The tool works by checking a given domain against known AI knowledge bases and citation patterns. You enter a domain, and the tool returns a score indicating how visible your brand is to AI systems — systems that are increasingly the first place people go for recommendations and answers.

## How to Check Your AI Visibility Score

### Step 1: Enter Your Domain

Visit [aivisibility.pro](https://aivisibility.pro) and type your website domain into the input field. No signup or API key is required for the basic check.

### Step 2: View Your AI Visibility Score

After submitting, the tool generates a score across the four major AI platforms. The score reflects both mention frequency and the prominence of those mentions in AI-generated responses.

### Step 3: Review Platform-by-Platform Breakdown

The results break down visibility per AI assistant, so you can see where your brand is well-known and where it may be underrepresented.

## Why AI Visibility Matters for Brands

Traditional search engine optimization targets Google, Bing, and other search engines. But AI assistants draw from different knowledge sources and citation patterns than search crawlers. A brand that ranks well on Google may be invisible to ChatGPT if it lacks sufficient cross-referenced content online.

This tool gives brand managers and marketers a way to audit their AI presence before competitors do. As AI assistants become the first interface people use to discover products, services, and companies, knowing where you stand with those systems becomes a strategic advantage.

## Practical Evaluation Checklist

- Enter a competitor domain to benchmark your own AI visibility against theirs
- Check the breakdown across all four AI assistants to identify which platforms need more coverage
- Recheck periodically to measure the impact of content campaigns or PR efforts
- Note that AI training data has a cutoff date — the tool reflects what AI systems have ingested, not real-time mentions

## Security Notes

AI Visibility is a client-side web tool. No credentials or personal data are required to use the basic service. As with any third-party tool that processes your domain name, avoid submitting domains associated with highly sensitive or internal-only infrastructure.

## FAQ

**Q: Is AI Visibility free to use?**
**A:** Yes, the basic domain check is free with no account required.

**Q: Does a high AI Visibility score mean my brand will be recommended by AI?**
**A:** Not necessarily. The score reflects mentions and citation patterns in AI training data. Being frequently mentioned does not guarantee a positive recommendation — it only indicates visibility.

**Q: How often should I check my AI visibility?**
**A:** AI models are retrained periodically, and your visibility can change after major model updates. Checking quarterly is a reasonable starting point.

**Q: Can I track AI visibility over time?**
**A:** The free tool provides point-in-time checks. Historical tracking requires recording scores manually or using a paid monitoring service.

**Q: Does AI Visibility work for local businesses?**
**A:** The tool focuses on domain-level analysis. Local businesses with strong online presence may score well, but the tool is primarily designed for brands with a web domain.
