## What Is Adadot?

Adadot positions itself as a "fitness tracker for developers." Unlike traditional engineering metrics tools that focus solely on velocity and output, Adadot correlates productivity data with developer well-being indicators.

The platform benchmarks your team's data against what it describes as 50,000 active developer datasets, giving context to raw numbers. The core premise: engineering leadership is a two-way street, and most tools extract data from developers without giving anything back.

Adadot aims to answer three questions engineering leaders constantly face:

- Are our developers overworked?
- Is there enough focus time for quality code?
- How likely are we to deliver this feature on time?

## Pricing

| Plan | Price | Key Features |

| Personal Free | $0 | Personal dashboards, 3 months data lookback |
| Personal Pro | $10/mo | Everything in Free plus personal goal setting (Missions) |
| Team | $20/mo | Team analytics, sharing permissions, team goals, up to 10 members |
| Enterprise | Contact | Granular permissions, Jira integration, team benchmarks, custom reporting, 10+ members |

The free tier is genuinely free with no time limit, making it viable for individual developers who want to track their own patterns.

## Integrations and Data Sources

Adadot connects to the tools developers already use:

- **GitHub** — pull request activity, review patterns, contribution frequency
- **Slack** — integration available, but Adadot states it never stores or processes Slack messages and does not access emails
- **Jira** — available on Enterprise plan for sprint and delivery tracking

The privacy model is worth noting: individual metrics are always aggregated and anonymized at the team level. Team-level metrics only become accessible when a team has three or more members, protecting individual privacy in small groups.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Create an account

Sign up at [adadot.com](https://adadot.com) with your work email. The free Personal tier requires no credit card.

### Step 2: Connect your integrations

From the dashboard, connect GitHub (required for baseline metrics). Add Slack for communication pattern insights. Enterprise users can add Jira for delivery tracking.

### Step 3: Review your baseline

After connecting sources, Adadot begins aggregating data. Within a few days, your personal dashboard shows initial benchmarks against the 50,000-developer dataset.

### Step 4: Set Missions (optional)

Personal Pro and Team plans include "Missions" — personal or team goal setting. Examples: reduce meeting time by 2 hours per week, increase uninterrupted focus blocks, maintain sustainable PR review turnaround.

### Step 5: Share team insights (Team plan and above)

Team analytics aggregate individual data into team-level patterns. Sharing permissions control who sees what. The platform is designed to build trust between developers and engineering leadership, not enable surveillance.

## Deeper Analysis

### What makes Adadot different from LinearB, Jellyfish, or Jira dashboards?

Most engineering analytics tools optimize for management visibility — they tell leaders what teams are doing. Adadot optimizes for developer self-awareness first, then rolls up to team insights.

The "fitness tracker" framing is deliberate. Like a Fitbit shows you your step count and sleep quality so you can make your own decisions, Adadot shows developers their focus time, meeting load, and contribution patterns so they can self-correct before burnout hits.

### The benchmarking angle

Raw metrics are meaningless without context. "4 hours of focus time per day" could be excellent or terrible depending on your role, team, and company stage. Adadot's 50,000-developer benchmark gives you percentile rankings, so you know whether your patterns are typical or outliers.

### Privacy as a feature

The explicit privacy commitments matter in a market where developer analytics tools have a reputation for enabling micromanagement. The three-person minimum for team metrics and the no-Slack-message-storage policy are concrete guardrails, not marketing language.

## Security Notes

Adadot's privacy model includes several concrete commitments:

- Slack messages are never stored or processed
- Emails are never accessed
- Individual metrics are aggregated and anonymized at the team level
- Team metrics require a minimum of three team members to be accessible

These are product design choices, not just policy statements. The three-person minimum prevents identifying individuals in small teams. The no-Slack-storage policy means communication content never enters Adadot's systems.

For enterprise deployments, contact Adadot for details on data residency, SSO, and compliance certifications — these are not documented on the public pricing page.

## Conclusion

Adadot occupies a specific niche: developer analytics that prioritize well-being alongside productivity. If your engineering organization is wrestling with burnout, unsustainable pace, or the tension between delivery pressure and developer happiness, Adadot offers a structured way to measure and address those concerns.

The free tier makes it low-risk to try individually. The Team plan at $20/month per seat is reasonable for small teams. Enterprise pricing requires contact, which is standard for B2B tools at that scale.

The key differentiator is the well-being-first framing. Most engineering analytics tools ask "how can we make teams faster?" Adadot asks "how can we make teams sustainable?" That's a meaningful distinction for organizations that have learned the hard way that velocity without well-being is a short-term game.
