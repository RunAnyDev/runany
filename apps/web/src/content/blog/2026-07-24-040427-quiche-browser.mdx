## What Is Quiche Browser?

Quiche Browser is an independently-built iOS web browser with a sharp focus on minimalism. Rather than adding features like tab syncing, extensions, or cloud tabs, Quiche Browser prioritizes a fast, distraction-free reading experience. It was funded entirely by its users through the App Store, with no venture capital or ads.

The app has been praised in the press for its craftsmanship. John Gruber of Daring Fireball called it "very robust, exquisitely designed, stunningly handsome," and noted it manages to be modern in appearance while maintaining a classic focus on serving the user.

## Setup and Installation

There is no setup required. Quiche Browser is installed directly from the iOS App Store:

1. Open the App Store on your iPhone or iPad
2. Search for "Quiche Browser"
3. Tap **Get** to install
4. Open the app and set Quiche Browser as your default browser if desired (Settings > Quiche Browser > Default Browser App)

No account creation, no sign-up flow, no data collection on first launch.

## Deeper Analysis

### What Makes It Different

Most iOS browsers fall into two camps: Safari clones that inherit Apple's limitations, or Chromium-based browsers that carry the weight of desktop Chrome's ecosystem. Quiche Browser sits outside both.

The developer builds and maintains it alone, which means no feature bloat driven by product managers or growth teams. Every decision traces back to a single question: does this serve the user?

### Privacy Approach

Because the app is funded directly by users rather than through advertising or data collection, the incentive structure is fundamentally different. There is no business model that benefits from knowing your browsing habits.

### Performance

With no extensions, no tab sync infrastructure, and no background processes beyond what's strictly necessary, Quiche Browser tends to be lighter on system resources than mainstream alternatives.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Quiche Browser open source?**
**A:** No. It is a proprietary app built and maintained by a single independent developer.

**Q: Can I sync tabs across devices?**
**A:** No. Quiche Browser intentionally omits cross-device sync to keep the app simple and avoid tying you to an account.

**Q: Does it support extensions?**
**A:** No. The developer has chosen not to include extension support to maintain performance and privacy.

**Q: How does the developer make money?**
**A:** Directly from App Store purchases. No ads, no data selling, no freemium model.

**Q: Is it available on Android?**
**A:** No. Quiche Browser is iOS and iPadOS only.
