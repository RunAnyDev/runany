## TL;DR

> **TL;DR:** Write It Down is a paid Google Sheets template that turns a spreadsheet into a personal finance tracker — showing your full month on one screen for $8.99 with no subscription.

## What Is Write It Down?

Write It Down is a Google Sheets-based personal finance tracker. Instead of a standalone app or service, you buy a spreadsheet template that lives in your own Google Drive. The sheet displays your entire month on a single view, surfacing spending patterns that most budgeting tools bury in menus and reports.

> "A Google Sheet you already know. Pay once and own forever."

The product has two versions:

- **Sheet v1** at $5.99 USD — income and expense tracking with your own categories
- **Sheet v2** at $8.99 USD — multi-currency, budgeting, up to 10 accounts, and 50 money tips

Over 2,674 users have purchased one of the two versions.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Purchase the Template

Visit [write-it-down.com](https://write-it-down.com) and choose your version. Payment is a one-time charge via Stripe — no subscription to forget about.

### Step 2: Open the Sheet

After purchase you receive a link to the live Google Sheet. You copy it to your own Google Drive. Sheet contents are never stored on the vendor's servers.

### Step 3: Connect Your Accounts

Enter transactions manually or connect multiple Sheets you already own. The v2 dashboard merges data across Sheets into multi-year wealth charts and category trends.

### Step 4: Review the Monthly View

The main sheet shows your full month on one screen. Transactions, category breakdowns, and budget targets are visible at a glance without navigating between tabs.

## Deeper Analysis

### What Makes It Different

Most budgeting apps either lock you into a subscription or require you to hand over bank credentials. Write It Down takes the opposite approach: it runs inside Google Sheets, a tool most people already use. There is no app to install, no account to maintain separately, and no recurring charge.

The product description says it plainly:

> "60% of adults cannot name what they spent last month."

The single-screen monthly view is the core UX argument. Rather than generating reports, the sheet is designed to make spending visible as you go.

### Dashboard (v2 Only)

The v2 upgrade includes a dashboard that merges multiple Sheets into charts tracking net worth over time and spending by category across months. This addresses a limitation of a single-sheet setup: you cannot easily compare March to April in one view without pulling data into a separate summary sheet.

This likely refers to the Google Workspace Marketplace app review process for add-ons, though the product functions as a regular shared Sheet template rather than an installable Sheets add-on.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this a Google Sheets add-on or a template?**
**A:** It is a Google Sheets template. You purchase a link, copy the Sheet to your own Drive, and use it as a regular spreadsheet. It is not installed from the Google Workspace Marketplace.

**Q: Does it sync with my bank automatically?**
**A:** No. Transactions are entered manually. The v2 dashboard can merge data from multiple Sheets you connect manually.

**Q: What happens if Write It Down shuts down?**
**A:** You own the Google Sheet permanently. Since it is just a spreadsheet, there is no service dependency. Your data stays in your Google Drive.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
**A:** There is an interactive demo on the product page you can open and explore before purchasing.

**Q: Does v2 work for multiple currencies?**
**A:** Yes, multi-currency support is a v2 feature. v1 tracks a single currency.

## Conclusion

Write It Down targets people who want the structure of a budgeting system without adopting a new app or paying ongoing fees. Its main value proposition — a full month visible on one screen — addresses the common failure mode of personal finance tools: out of sight, out of mind.

The one-time pricing model is honest and straightforward. The product has over 2,600 users and runs entirely inside Google Sheets, which means there is no vendor lock-in for your transaction data.

If you already use Google Sheets and want a low-commitment way to see where your money goes, the $8.99 v2 is the complete package. If you just need basic income and expense tracking, v1 at $5.99 covers the essentials.
