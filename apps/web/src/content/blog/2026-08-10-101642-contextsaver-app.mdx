## What Is Context Saver?

Long AI conversations are fragile. Re-explaining your stack, requirements, or preferences every time you return to a project wastes time and breaks flow. Context Saver is a lightweight Chrome extension that solves this by letting you save, bookmark, and restore chat contexts across sessions.

The core product is the **Context Bubble** — a bookmarkable snapshot of a conversation's key context. You can organize bubbles visually, restore them with one click, and share them with teammates.

> No account is required to get started. The free plan gives you three context bubbles with manual bookmarking for ChatGPT.

### Step 2: Pin the Extension

After installation, pin the Context Saver icon to your Chrome toolbar for quick access.

### Step 3: Save Your First Context Bubble

1. Have an active conversation in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.
2. Click the Context Saver icon in your toolbar.
3. Click **"Save"** to bookmark the current conversation context.
4. Give it a label so you can find it later.

### Step 4: Restore a Saved Context

1. Click the Context Saver icon.
2. Select the bubble you want to restore.
3. The context is restored to your active chat session.

### Step 5: Export and Share

Context Saver lets you export and import bubble libraries. This is useful for:
- Backing up your context collection
- Sharing context snapshots across devices
- Onboarding teammates to ongoing projects
