> **TL;DR:** Foster showed up on Hacker News in 2022 as on-demand editing for your writing. It is now a members-only collective built around invite-only 90-minute Zoom writing circles, and a $999 four-week cohort is the paid step. The product is a schedule, not a piece of software.

## What it is

Foster is a co-operated collective of writers, editors, coaches, and facilitators, built around one recurring ritual: the Writing Circle. The core team is five Stewards supported by sixteen Contributors. The name most likely to land is Dan Hunt, previously head editor of an activist publication with 700K readers and now writing Storyless, a newsletter on narrative design.

A circle runs 90 minutes on Zoom. The application form describes the format more precisely than the marketing does: drop into a creative flow state together, write for 60 minutes, then share your progress. That form also states the sessions are invite-only.

```text
Tuesday 9 am - 10:30 am EST with Minnow Park
Wednesday 9 am - 10:30 am EST with Dylan Tweney
Thursday 11 am - 12:30 pm EST with Sara Campbell
```

Three weekly slots, all in Eastern time, and you commit to one of them for most weeks. Facilitators work with meditation, somatic experiencing, and breathwork to get the group into that state before anyone writes a word.

## Why it matters

Most writing software sells throughput. You write a paragraph, it suggests three more, you accept the best one, you publish. The unit of value is words produced.

Foster assumes the opposite, that the hard part of writing is staying present rather than producing output. The thing being improved is your own attention, which is why there is no prompt library and no model picker anywhere in the product.

The cost of that assumption is that the good stuff happens at a fixed hour on a weekday, on Zoom, with people you have not met. If that sounds like a feature, this is worth a look. If it sounds like a constraint, keep reading.

## The three tiers

| Tier | Price | Length | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Writing Circle | Free, invite-only | 90 minutes, weekly | Live facilitated session and peer share-back |
| Cohort | $999 | Four weeks | Circles, editing network, coaching, expert workshops |
| Membership | Not published | Ongoing | You apply after finishing a cohort |

Every cohort carries a 100% money-back guarantee, and you choose how deep to go. The floor is the opening and closing ceremonies, 90 minutes each. Cohorts start at the top of the month, so you are joining a rhythm that is already running.

## Trying it without paying

The free tier is the application form for a Writing Circle. It asks for your name, email, and website, then has you pick one of the three weekly slots you could commit to. No card and no trial, and if you are not selected you have lost a form submission.

That is the entire funnel before you spend anything. Once you have done a free circle, the paid step is a Cohort.

## What a cohort actually looks like

A cohort is four weeks of committed practice around one thread, which is Foster's term for a creative question, complexity, or muse that is alive for you. Cohort 12, titled Cultivating Creative Capacity, started on October 1 and was facilitated by Kelly Wilde Miller, who wrote the first draft of her book Creative Dysregulation in five days and has since worked with two dozen first-time authors on their own books.

The menu of modalities Foster runs inside a cohort:

```text
Meditation and mindful writing
Somatic experiencing and writing
Peer writing coaching
Peer review and revision
Relational writing
Professional editing
Concentric circling
```

Relational writing and concentric circling are the two entries that need explaining. Relational writing means writing with and for specific people rather than in general. Concentric circling shares the work outward in widening rings, so feedback arrives from further and further away until you are reading to the room.

## What to watch for

The $999 is the number everyone fixates on, and it is the cheaper half. A cohort takes a month of live calls on top of it, and membership after that asks for more of the same on a schedule Foster does not publish publicly.

Retreats are the part that is hard to price. Foster meets in person twice a year, upstate New York in summer and warmer places such as Mexico and Costa Rica in winter. The site describes them as a core practice alongside the circles but publishes no cost, so budget for them separately rather than assuming the cohort fee covers travel.

There is also a pivot worth understanding. The 2022 launch pitch was on-demand editing, and editing still exists, but it now arrives through a Foster Chrome extension inside a community structure rather than as the headline product. Anyone arriving for a proofreading tool should know that before they apply.

Everything runs on Zoom and WhatsApp. That is a deliberately low-friction choice, and it also means the quality of any given session depends heavily on who else is in the room.
