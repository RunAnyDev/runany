## What Is StartupLaunchDay?

StartupLaunchDay is a curated directory and news aggregator for the startup ecosystem. It surfaces daily product launches, trending topics, and funding rounds in one place — replacing the need to monitor multiple launch platforms, VC newsletters, and social feeds.

Key features:

- **Daily launches feed** — New products submitted and approved each day
- **Funding tracking** — Recent rounds tagged by stage and sector
- **Topic trends** — What's gaining traction among founders and investors
- **Startup listing** — Submit your own launch and get discovered

## Setup Workflow

No setup required — StartupLaunchDay is a web-first tool.

### Step 1: Browse the Launches Feed

Visit [startuplaunchday.com](https://startuplaunchday.com) and scroll the homepage feed. Launches are sorted by recency with category tags.

### Step 2: Filter by Category

Use the sidebar filters to narrow by:

- **Product type** — SaaS, dev tools, mobile apps, AI products
- **Funding stage** — Pre-seed, seed, Series A+
- **Sector** — Fintech, healthtech, developer tools, etc.

### Step 3: Submit Your Startup

Click **List Your Startup** to submit a new launch. You'll need:

- Product name and one-line description
- Website URL
- Category and target audience
- Optional: demo video or screenshots

Submissions go through a brief approval process before appearing in the feed.

## Deeper Analysis

### What Makes It Different

Unlike general news aggregators, StartupLaunchDay is laser-focused on the launch moment — the24-48 hour window when a product is most visible. This makes it useful for:

- **Founders** scouting competitor launches
- **Investors** spotting early signals in a sector
- **Builders** getting inspired by what's working

### Data Freshness

Launches appear within hours of submission. The funding section pulls from public sources and updates daily.

### Limitations

- Heavy on early-stage startups; limited coverage of Series B+
- Curation is light — quality varies
- No API access for programmatic querying

## FAQ

**Q: Is StartupLaunchDay free to use?**
**A:** Yes, browsing and listing are free. There may be paid promotion options for startups wanting extra visibility.

**Q: How is this different from Product Hunt?**
**A:** Product Hunt is curated and community-voted. StartupLaunchDay is more of a news feed with lighter curation — it moves faster and covers more early-stage experiments.

**Q: Can I track funding for a specific sector?**
**A:** Yes, use the sector filter on the funding page to narrow by fintech, healthtech, dev tools, etc.
