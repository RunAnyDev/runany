## What Is ProjectionLab?

ProjectionLab is a web-based personal finance simulator focused on retirement and financial independence planning. Rather than requiring users to build their own spreadsheet models, it provides interactive tools to project portfolio growth, withdrawal strategies, and tax implications over time.

The product targets three groups:
- **Early retirement seekers** planning FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) paths
- **Indie developers** evaluating the financial viability of going solo
- **General users** who want a clearer picture of their financial future beyond basic calculators

The product launched on Hacker News in July 2023, reaching 766 points and generating widespread discussion among the developer community. By 2026, it crossed $1M ARR with zero external funding — a rare bootstrapping outcome for a B2C SaaS in the personal finance space.

## Setup Workflow

ProjectionLab is a fully hosted web application. There is no self-hosted option or installation step required.

### Step 1: Create an Account

Navigate to [projectionlab.com](https://projectionlab.com) and sign up. The service offers a free tier with limited projections, and a paid tier for full access to all features including Monte Carlo simulations, tax optimization, and multi-account aggregation.

### Step 2: Connect Accounts (Optional)

ProjectionLab supports linking external financial accounts for automatic portfolio tracking:
- Brokerage accounts (Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard, etc.)
- Retirement accounts (401k, IRA, Roth IRA)
- Bank accounts for cash flow tracking

Manual entry is also supported if you prefer not to connect.

### Step 3: Configure Your Scenario

1. Set your current portfolio value and asset allocation
2. Define income sources (salary, side income, freelance)
3. Set withdrawal rate assumptions
4. Choose projection period (e.g., 30–50 years)
5. Run the simulation

### Step 4: Analyze Results

The dashboard displays:
- **Probability distributions** showing range of outcomes
- **Withdrawal sustainability** over time
- **Tax burden** estimates under different scenarios
- **Monte Carlo simulations** for market volatility

## Deeper Analysis

### Why ProjectionLab Stands Out

Most personal finance tools either oversimplify (basic compound interest calculators) or require significant spreadsheet expertise. ProjectionLab occupies a middle ground — accessible enough for a developer with no finance background, but detailed enough to model real-world complexity like tax drag, sequence-of-returns risk, and variable withdrawal strategies.

### Key Features

- **Monte Carlo simulation** — runs thousands of market scenarios to give probability ranges rather than a single point estimate
- **Tax-aware withdrawal sequencing** — optimizes which accounts to draw from in which order to minimize tax liability
- **Social Security / pension integration** — models guaranteed income streams alongside portfolio withdrawals
- **What-if scenario comparison** — compare multiple plans side by side (e.g., "retire at 45 vs. 55")
- **Mobile-responsive** — works on desktop and mobile browsers, no native app required

### Business Model

ProjectionLab is bootstrapped — $1M ARR with zero VC funding as of 2026. The founder has written publicly about the journey on the company blog, sharing revenue milestones and conversion metrics. This is notable in a space dominated by established players like Personal Capital (now Empower) and YNAB, both of which took external funding.

### Pricing

The free tier includes basic projection tools. Paid plans unlock full Monte Carlo analysis, tax optimization, and multi-scenario comparison. Exact pricing is available on the [pricing page](https://projectionlab.com/pricing).

## Security Notes

- Financial data is sensitive. Review ProjectionLab's [privacy policy](https://projectionlab.com/privacy) before connecting real accounts.
- If connecting via Plaid or similar aggregation, check what data is shared and for how long.
- For the highest security, use manual entry for portfolio values rather than account linking.
- Two-factor authentication is recommended on the linked financial accounts themselves, regardless of ProjectionLab's own security posture.

## FAQ

**Q: Is ProjectionLab open source?**
**A:** No. It is a proprietary SaaS product. The codebase is not publicly available.

**Q: Can I self-host ProjectionLab?**
**A:** No. There is no self-hosted option. It runs entirely in the browser as a hosted service.

**Q: How does ProjectionLab compare to Engage_WRITE or Personal Capital?**
**A:** ProjectionLab is focused specifically on retirement and FIRE projection modeling with Monte Carlo simulation. Personal Capital (now Empower) is more of a full-suite wealth management tool with net worth tracking, investment fees analysis, and advisor matching. YNAB is a budgeting tool. ProjectionLab does not replace either — it complements them by providing deeper projection modeling.

**Q: Does it support non-US users?**
**A:** The tool is primarily designed for US tax and investment structures. Non-US users should verify that the tax models apply to their country's system before relying on projections.

**Q: Is there an API?**
**A:** As of the last check, ProjectionLab does not expose a public API.
