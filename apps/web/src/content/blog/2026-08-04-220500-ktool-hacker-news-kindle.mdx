## TL;DR

> **TL;DR:** KTool is a free browser tool that sends Hacker News threads to your Kindle with one click — no account needed.

## What Is KTool?

KTool is a web-based tool for sending online content to your Kindle. The specific HN feature lets you paste any Hacker News thread URL and have it reformatted for E-ink reading and delivered directly to your Kindle (or downloaded as EPUB).

The project was born from a personal need. Creator Daniel Nguyen shared in the original HN launch thread:

> "I've been wearing glasses since I was 5. Since then I've been very conscious about time spent on computer screens. That's when I started using Kindle-related products to offload as many reading materials as possible. I was a happy customer of Push to Kindle. Then I ran into multiple limitations which led me to build KTool."

## How It Works

### Step 1: Open Any HN Thread

Navigate to any Hacker News discussion you want to read — a Show HN launch, an Ask HN thread, or just a thread you want to read carefully.

### Step 2: Send to Kindle with One Click

Paste the URL into KTool's web interface. The tool fetches the thread, reformats it for E-ink displays (clean typography, no ads, no distractions), and sends it to your registered Kindle email address.

### Step 3: Read on Your Kindle

The article appears on your Kindle overnight or within minutes. Your highlights and notes sync back to your note-taking apps.

## Features

**HN thread to Kindle:** Convert any Hacker News discussion into a clean, readable format optimized for E-ink screens.

**No account required:** Unlike most services, KTool does not require sign-up for the basic HN-to-Kindle feature. You only need a Kindle.

**EPUB download:** If you do not own a Kindle, you can download the thread as an EPUB file and read it in any EPUB-compatible reader.

**Notes and highlights sync:** Any annotations you make are automatically synced to your favorite note-taking apps.

**Beyond HN:** KTool also handles blog posts, Twitter threads, RSS feeds, and newsletters — making it a general "send anything to Kindle" tool.

## Use Cases

- **Deep reading:** Hacker News threads can be dense. Reading on Kindle removes screen fatigue and lets you work through complex technical discussions at your own pace.
- **Commute reading:** Queue up several HN threads on Sunday evening for the week ahead.
- **Offline access:** HN is readable on planes, subways, or anywhere without reliable connectivity.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need a Kindle to use this?**
**A:** No. If you do not have a Kindle, KTool offers an EPUB download option so you can read HN threads in any EPUB-compatible reader.

**Q: Is this free?**
**A:** Yes. The HN-to-Kindle feature is free and does not require an account. KTool also supports general web-to-Kindle functionality beyond HN.

**Q: Does it work with all HN thread types?**
**A:** It works with standard HN threads, Ask HN, Show HN, and discussions with up to hundreds of comments. Long threads are paginated or trimmed to fit Kindle's content limits.

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
**A:** Typically within minutes. KTool sends the content to your Kindle email address (associated with your Amazon account).
