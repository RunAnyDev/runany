## What Is BitBoard?

BitBoard builds AI agents that handle repetitive administrative tasks in healthcare clinics — filling out intake forms, prepping patient charts, managing referrals, and reconciling records. Unlike traditional RPA tools that break when EHR interfaces change, BitBoard embeds deterministic verification checks into each workflow so teams can confirm tasks were completed correctly before moving forward.

The product sits between a clinic's standard operating procedures and the actual EHR systems staff use daily. Clinics describe their workflows in plain language (for example: "prep a patient chart from these records before a visit"), and BitBoard turns those SOPs into agents that log into EHRs, navigate internal tools, and execute tasks in the background.

BitBoard's agents are designed to act like remote contractors — they operate asynchronously, don't require clinicians to babysit them, and produce auditable output. One early customer, an obesity medicine group, automated intake form data entry that previously consumed 30% of their medical assistants' time. The team onboarded and automated that workflow in under a week.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Submit Clinic SOPs

A clinic documents its standard workflows — intake procedures, scheduling follow-ups, referral management. These SOPs are written in natural language, not pseudocode.

### Step 2: Agent Generation

BitBoard converts SOPs into AI agents configured to operate against specific EHR interfaces and clinic tools. Agents include verification checkpoints that confirm the task completed as expected before marking it done.

### Step 3: Agent Execution

Agents operate in the background, logging into EHRs and completing tasks. Results are audit-logged. If a verification check fails, the agent flags the task for human review rather than silently skipping it.

### Step 4: Review and Iterate

Clinics review completed tasks through a minimal interface. BitBoard's team adjusts agent behavior based on feedback until workflows run reliably.

## Technical Challenges in Healthcare Automation

The BitBoard team, which includes former Forward employees, calls out three hard problems specific to automating healthcare work:

**Unreliable interfaces.** Many EHRs and clinic tools don't follow modern web standards. BitBoard has forked browser-use to handle the quirks of legacy health software. They're also building infrastructure for desktop-based and API-driven automation across a wide range of health systems.

**Verification.** In clinical settings, accuracy matters. BitBoard embeds deterministic checks into workflows rather than relying on probabilistic outputs alone. This lets clinics confirm the agent completed a task correctly before moving to the next step.

**Workflow generation from natural language.** Clinic SOPs are written for humans, not machines, and vary widely between organizations. Mapping these to reliable agent behavior is an ongoing engineering challenge.

## Security and Compliance

BitBoard operates under a HIPAA-compliant framework with audit logging. The product is designed for zero-retention environments where patient data is not stored unless auditing requires it. Clinics that need to move fast on compliance without building internal tooling can deploy BitBoard's agents under signed business associate agreements.
