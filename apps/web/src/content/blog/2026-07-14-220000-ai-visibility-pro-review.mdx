## What Is AI Visibility Pro?

AI Visibility Pro is a web-based SEO tool purpose-built for GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) — the practice of making your website more readable and citable by AI answer engines. Unlike traditional SEO tools that optimize for Google search rankings, AI Visibility Pro audits your site against the data needs of AI systems: crawlability, entity clarity, claim verification, and trust signals.

The free tier lets you audit one URL and get a surface-level score. Paid tiers (starting at $9.90 for 25 credits) unlock full-site crawls, brand entity profiles, P0/P1/P2 fix roadmaps, and competitor gap analysis across six major AI platforms.

## Key Features

**Full-site crawl inventory.** Audits homepage, product, pricing, case studies, docs, help center, blog, sitemap, robots.txt, PDFs, and hidden entry points for AI accessibility.

**Brand entity profile.** Maps who you are, what you offer, product boundaries, aliases, target customers, use cases, and disambiguation risks — critical for AI systems that disambiguate entities.

**Claim-level evidence ledger.** Tracks every important claim on your site, maps it to a source, verifies freshness, and flags citation readiness.

**AI intent coverage matrix.** Checks whether your site answers the full range of queries AI systems route to answer engines: recommendation, comparison, alternative, tutorial, pricing, risk, trust, and buying queries.

**Technical crawlability audit.** Finds blockers in robots directives, sitemap quality, canonical tags, status codes, JavaScript rendering, PDF text extraction, image alt text, and internal link structure.

**Schema and structured data recommendations.** Suggests Organization, Product, Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb, Article, and CaseStudy markup opportunities that work without over-relying on invisible structured data.

**Trust signal gap analysis.** Reviews source blocks, update dates, authorship, company proof, certifications, compliance badges, customer evidence, contact details, and correction paths.

**Competitor and off-site evidence map.** Compares where competitors have stronger public proof across media mentions, reports, partners, communities, and app stores.

## Setup Workflow

There is no installation — AI Visibility Pro is a fully web-based SaaS tool.

**Step 1: Run a free URL audit**

Visit [ai-visibility.pro](https://ai-visibility.pro) and enter your website URL. The free tier gives you a surface-level AI visibility score for that single URL.

**Step 2: Review the audit report**

The report covers six categories: crawlability, brand entity clarity, claim evidence, AI intent coverage, technical structure, and trust signals. Each finding includes a severity rating (P0/P1/P2) and a specific action item.

**Step 3: Purchase credits for full-site coverage**

For full-site audits across all pages, buy a credit pack from the pricing page. Credits unlock the full command center view, competitor comparisons, and the off-site evidence map.

## Security Notes

AI Visibility Pro accesses your website as a crawler (similar to Googlebot). The service does not require API keys or login credentials for the free URL audit. For authenticated pages or private content behind login, those routes will not be audited unless you explicitly grant crawler access.
