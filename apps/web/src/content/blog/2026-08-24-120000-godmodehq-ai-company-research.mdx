## What Is GodModeHQ?

GodModeHQ is a browser-based prospecting platform that embeds AI agents directly into a spreadsheet interface. Instead of buying a list of prospects and sending the same template to all of them, you feed the agent a company URL and ask it questions — "What are their recent feature launches?", "Do they have SOC Type 2 certification?", "What pain points can we solve?" — and the agent fills in the answers column by column.

The founder, Mert, described the core problem on HN:

> "The sales landscape is pretty crowded. Most solutions focus on sending the same email or LinkedIn message to thousands of people. This approach worked between 2010 and 2020 at best, however it became such a big habit that everyone started doing it."

Personalization with AI typically stops at surface-level facts (where someone graduated, a photo they shared on Instagram). GodModeHQ goes deeper — the agents recognize your target company's website, embed your ICP and context, and surface structured intelligence you can act on before reaching out.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Create a Free Account

Visit [godmodehq.com](https://godmodehq.com) and sign up. The platform runs in the browser — no install required.

### Step 2: Connect Your Email and LinkedIn

Link your email provider and LinkedIn account so the agent can draft outreach messages and you can send them without switching tools.

### Step 3: Import or Add Prospects

Add companies by URL or import a list. Each row represents a target company.

### Step 4: Ask the Agent Questions

For each row, the agent recognizes the company and starts filling columns based on your questions. Examples from the HN launch:

- "What are some of the pain points we can solve for the company?"
- "Does the company have SOC Type 2 certification?"
- "What are the recent feature launches they did?"

### Step 5: Review and Send Outreach

The platform encourages reviewing the agent's findings before sending. Outreach can be sent directly via the connected email or LinkedIn account.

## Deeper Analysis

### How the Agent Architecture Works

The agents are presented as "columns" in the spreadsheet — each column is a running AI agent specialized for a type of research question. This is described as "Perplexity integrated into a spreadsheet."

The agent reads the target company's website directly, combines that with your ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) context, and produces structured output in the cell. Multiple agents can run in parallel across different columns for the same row (company).

### What It Does Not Do

GodModeHQ deliberately avoids:
- Domain warmup services
- High-volume sending (10k emails per day cold outreach)
- Generic spray-and-pray templates

The design philosophy is to slow down the outreach process and ensure the sender has actually read and understood what the agent uncovered.
