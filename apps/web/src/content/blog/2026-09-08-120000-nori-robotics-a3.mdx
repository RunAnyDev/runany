## What Is Nori A3?

Nori A3 is an affordable bimanual robot designed for everyday home and office tasks. The company, Nori Robotics, positions it as the most capable robot at its price point — $1,688 full price, no deposit required, ships fall 2026.

The robot features two 7+1 degree-of-freedom arms with 1.5kg payload capacity each, a 12-meter lidar sensor with 8-12Hz scanning, four 720p RGB cameras mounted across the grippers, head, and neck, plus a speaker and microphone for voice commands. Battery life is rated at 6–8 hours.

Key tasks the robot handles include kitchen assistance, tidying, fetching items from the fridge, loading dishes, folding clothes, and pouring ingredients.

The company also operates a Skills Marketplace where users can train the robot at home and share learned skills with other Nori owners.
