## What Is Aircada?

Aircada is a Shopify app that adds HTML5-based 3D product configurators to eCommerce product pages. When installed, shoppers can rotate, zoom, and customize products — changing colors, materials, engravings, or other options — directly on the product page without loading a separate app or leaving the page.

The product positions itself as a no-code alternative to building custom 3D visualization tooling. Rather than requiring a developer to integrate Three.js or a 3D rendering pipeline, merchants add a block to their Shopify theme and the configurator renders on the product page automatically.

Supported niches include:

- Sports and Outdoors (helmets, equipment)
- Apparel and Accessories (jewelry, bags)
- Jewelry and Luxury goods
- Home and Furniture
- Custom Merchandise (mugs, prints)
- Gaming and Tech (PC cases, accessories)

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Install the Shopify App

Aircada is distributed as a Shopify app. From the [pricing page](https://aircada.com/pricing), click **Install App** and approve the Shopify permissions. The app connects to your Shopify store and creates the necessary theme blocks.

### Step 2: Add the Configurator Block to Your Theme

After installation, Aircada adds a **Theme Block** to Shopify's Customizer. Go to your Shopify theme editor:

1. Open any product page in the Shopify Customizer
2. Add the **Aircada Configurator** block to the product template
3. Select which product variants should trigger the 3D viewer

No code edits are required. Shopify's native theme block system handles the integration.

### Step 3: Configure Product Options

In the Aircada merchant dashboard, map each Shopify product variant to its corresponding 3D material or color option. When a shopper selects a variant in Shopify, the 3D view updates to reflect that choice in real-time.

### Step 4: Publish

Once configured, the 3D configurator appears automatically on the product page for shoppers browsing your store on desktop and mobile.

## Pricing

Aircada uses a **flat-rate transaction fee model** rather than recurring monthly subscriptions. Fees apply per successful sale through the configurator:

- **Free tier**: No monthly fee. Aircada takes a per-transaction cut on sales made through the 3D configurator.
- **Pro and Scale tiers**: Higher volume tiers reduce the per-transaction fee as your sales grow.

The exact fee percentages are not publicly listed on the website — interested merchants need to request access or check the Shopify app listing for current rates.

## Security Notes

Aircada runs as a Shopify app and operates within Shopify's permission model. The 3D viewer loads assets from Aircada's CDN — verify with your security team whether external CDN asset loading is within your store's content security policy before going live.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Aircada require WebGL or a browser plugin?**
**A:** No. The configurator is HTML5-based and runs in standard mobile and desktop browsers without plugins.

**Q: Can I use Aircada with a custom Shopify theme?**
**A:** Yes, as long as your theme supports Shopify's Theme Block system (available on most modern Shopify themes). Older themes may require manual integration.

**Q: Does Aircada work for stores outside of Shopify?**
**A:** Currently the product is focused on Shopify merchants. A WooCommerce or standalone embed version is not listed on the website.

**Q: Are 3D model files uploaded to Aircada's servers?**
**A:** Product 3D assets are hosted on Aircada's CDN and served to shoppers through the configurator. Consult the privacy policy for details on asset storage and retention.

## Conclusion

Aircada fills a specific gap in Shopify eCommerce: merchants who want interactive 3D product visualization without hiring a 3D developer or subscribing to an enterprise CMS. The flat-rate fee model is attractive for growing stores, though the lack of public pricing makes it harder to compare against alternatives upfront.

If you run a Shopify store selling customizable physical products, Aircada is worth a trial — especially if your current product pages rely on static images to show variant options.
