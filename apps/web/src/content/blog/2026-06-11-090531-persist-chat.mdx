## What Is Persist?

Persist is a cold outreach AI agent that handles the full sales funnel from prospect discovery to multi-channel follow-up. Instead of manually searching LinkedIn or buying prospect lists, you give Persist your ideal customer profile and it finds matching leads across 200 million+ LinkedIn profiles, X (Twitter), and email records.

The core value prop: it does not stop until someone replies. If an email goes cold, it pivots to LinkedIn. If LinkedIn gets no response, it tries X. The multi-channel persistence is the differentiator.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Connect Your Accounts

Persist requires OAuth connections to:
- **LinkedIn** – for profile data and outreach
- **X (Twitter)** – for social reach
- **Email** (Gmail or Outlook) – for email sequences

The onboarding wizard walks through each connection. No API keys to manage manually.

### Step 2: Define Your ICP

You specify:
- Industry, company size, job title
- Geography and language
- Pain points or product use case

This feeds into the prospect scoring engine.

### Step 3: Launch Your Campaign

Once your ICP is set, Persist begins:
1. Finding matching profiles across LinkedIn and X
2. Enriching with verified email addresses
3. Sending the first outreach sequence
4. Tracking opens, clicks, and replies

### Step 4: Monitor in the Dashboard

The dashboard shows:
- Total prospects in pipeline
- Reply rate per channel
- Active follow-up sequences
- Revenue attribution per campaign

## Deeper Analysis

### Multi-Channel Sequencing

Persist starts with email as the primary channel. If no reply after N days, it escalates to LinkedIn InMail. Still cold? It moves to X DMs. This layered approach improves deliverability and reduces the risk of any single channel getting rate-limited or flagged.

### 200M+ LinkedIn Profile Database

The prospect database is the backbone. Persist claims coverage across 200 million+ LinkedIn profiles, making it competitive with tools like Apollo.io or RocketReach for breadth. The enrichment layer pulls in work history, current company, and seniority level.

### AI-Generated Personalized Copy

Each outreach message is generated per prospect using the product description and ICP context. The agent does not send generic templates — it references specific details from the prospect's profile.

## Security Notes

- OAuth connections mean Persist holds tokens, not passwords — you can revoke access at any time
- Outreach activity lives on Persist's servers; ensure your company's data handling policy covers this
- LinkedIn's Terms of Service restrict automated outreach — use with caution and at your own risk

## FAQ

**Q: How does Persist find email addresses?**
**A:** It cross-references its database of 200M+ LinkedIn profiles with email discovery signals. No guarantees on deliverability — email verification is included.

**Q: Can I write my own outreach templates?**
**A:** The default is AI-generated, but you can provide custom templates or tone guidelines per campaign.

**Q: Does it work for B2B and B2C?**
**A:** Primarily B2B — the LinkedIn focus and ICP targeting are built for business outreach.
