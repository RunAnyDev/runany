## What Is Vostorq?

Most team tools optimize for speed. Chat is fast. Email is fast. Video calls are fast. But fast does not mean durable — the reasoning behind a decision vanishes into a channel scroll, a meeting recording nobody watches, or a document that explains what changed without capturing why.

Vostorq takes the opposite bet. Its core premise: **important discussions should form the source of truth for your company**, not sit as an afterthought beside where the actual work happens.

The product is built around two primitives:

- **Spaces** — persistent homes for long-running areas of work: a product team, an engineering function, a customer account, a strategic initiative.
- **Topics** — focused discussions inside a Space, each scoped to a single decision, proposal, debate, RFC, incident, or policy question.

The result is company knowledge that compounds rather than scatters. When a new engineer joins, they can find the Architecture decision Topic from six months ago and understand not just what was decided but what was debated and traded off along the way.

## Setup Workflow

Vostorq is a hosted SaaS product — no self-hosting or infrastructure setup required.

### Step 1: Create a Workspace

Navigate to [app.vostorq.com](https://app.vostorq.com) and create a workspace. You can start with the free Trial plan (1 month, up to 100 Spaces, no domain auto-join) or jump straight to Pro.

### Step 2: Invite Your Team

Invite team members by email. The Pro plan includes domain auto-join — anyone with an email address on your verified domain can automatically access the workspace without a manual invitation.

### Step 3: Create Your First Spaces

Think about your team's operating areas. Common starting points:

- Product Strategy
- Engineering Architecture
- Customer Escalations
- Leadership Operations

Each Space gets a clean, focused view of all the Topics inside it.

### Step 4: Open a Topic

Inside a Space, open a Topic for a specific discussion. Topics are not threaded chat — they are structured conversations scoped to one question. You can:

- Post messages and replies
- Attach files
- Tag team members
- Watch or favorite topics to get notified of updates

### Step 5: Connect AI Agents (Pro)

Pro and Enterprise plans include AI Agents. These can monitor Topics, surface relevant context, and help turn a running discussion into a summarized decision record.

## Deeper Analysis

### The Problem Vostorq Is Solving

Chat apps were designed for quick coordination. They are good at it. But quick coordination leaves a shallow trace — messages pile up, threads drift, and the decisions made in a channel become archaeological artifacts that require someone to have been there at the time.

Docs and wikis solve the durability problem but lose the reasoning. A document that says "we decided to use Postgres" preserves the answer but not the debate about Postgres versus MySQL, the tradeoffs discussed, or the objections raised.

Vostorq's positioning is explicitly that there is a third category of work — discussions too dynamic for a doc and too important to disappear — and that category deserves its own tool.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Vostorq open source?**
**A:** No. Vostorq is a proprietary SaaS product operated by Oktona LLC. There is no self-hostable or open-source version at this time.

**Q: How does Vostorq compare to Slack?**
**A:** Slack is optimized for quick, ephemeral coordination. Vostorq is designed for discussions that need to persist and be searchable later. They serve different purposes — Vostorq explicitly says to keep lightweight coordination wherever it already happens and use Vostorq for discussions that should stay useful.

**Q: Does Vostorq have a free plan?**
**A:** There is no permanent free plan, but the Trial plan is free for 1 month with full Pro features (up to 100 Spaces). After that, you need a paid plan to continue.

**Q: What are AI Agents in Vostorq?**
**A:** AI Agents are a Pro/Enterprise feature that helps monitor Topics, surface relevant context, and assist in turning ongoing discussions into structured knowledge. The exact capabilities are only visible inside the product — the website describes it as helping "turn discussion into company knowledge."

**Q: Can I use Vostorq for customer-facing discussions?**
**A:** Yes. The product supports customer escalations as a use case, with Private and Group Conversations available on all plans for sensitive context.
