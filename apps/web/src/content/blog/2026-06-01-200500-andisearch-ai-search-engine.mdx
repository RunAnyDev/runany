## What Is Andisearch?

Andisearch is a privacy-first AI search engine built by a team of journalists and engineers who watched the media industry collapse under Google's ad dominance. Instead of returning a ranked list of links like traditional search, Andi gives you direct answers through a conversational AI assistant — think "search + chat GPT" in one interface.

The core pitch: Google search is dying for anyone searching in specialized domains like finance, health, or technical topics. Results are buried under spam farms, clickbait, and content marketing. Andi fights back with server-side spam blacklists, encrypted POST-based queries that bypass browser history, and an AI that actually reads and synthesizes the content it finds.

Unlike Perplexity or ChatGPT Search, Andi is completely free with no usage limits, no registration required, and no extension or app to install. It is a web app you open in any browser.

## Setup Workflow

There is nothing to install.

### Step 1: Open the Site

Navigate to [https://andisearch.com](https://andisearch.com). The interface loads immediately — no sign-up, no paywall, no captcha gate.

### Step 2: Enter a Query

Type a question or topic into the search box. Andi interprets natural language queries and breaks them down intelligently. For complex questions, it generates "deep answers" — synthesized responses that cite and link to sources.

### Step 3: Explore Results

Results display in a visual card-based layout by default. You can switch to a simple list view. Each result includes a proxied Reader View that strips ads and clutter so you can preview the actual content safely.

### Step 4: Use Spam Fighting Tools

Built-in tools let you blacklist domains locally and report spam. These are applied server-side for all users. If a result slipped through the blacklist, you can report it directly from the search result card.

## Deeper Analysis

### The Interface

Andisearch looks nothing like Google. The default view shows answer cards in a grid with a clean visual hierarchy — no sponsored results, no sidebar clutter. The AI chat panel sits inline with results, letting you ask follow-up questions without losing context.

The conversational flow is closer to a smart research assistant than a traditional search engine. Ask "What caused the 2008 financial crisis?" and you get a synthesized answer with key points and linked sources, not just a wall of links.

### Privacy Model

Andi encrypts queries as POST requests — they never touch browser history. No tracking pixels, no cross-site cookies, no account required to search. The team explicitly cites privacy as a core differentiator from ad-supported search engines.

### Spam Fighting

The founders have journalism backgrounds and watched spam content destroy legitimate media. Server-side spam blacklists are applied before results are served. Users can also maintain personal blacklists and report new spam finds. This is a genuine differentiator in a landscape where Google's quality issues in specialized verticals are well-documented.

### Search Quality

In testing, Andisearch performed well on complex multi-part questions. The deep answer synthesis correctly identified key concepts and cited sources. On straightforward informational queries, it matched or exceeded Perplexity's conciseness. The reader-view proxy for content preview is a practical touch — safe preview without clicking through to ad-laden pages.

## FAQ

**Q:** Is Andisearch free to use?
**A:** Yes, completely free with no usage limits. No account required.

**Q:** How is this different from Perplexity or ChatGPT Search?
**A:** Andisearch is a standalone web app with no login required, no tiered pricing, and explicit spam-fighting tools. The interface prioritizes a clean, ad-free result experience over a chatbot persona.

**Q:** Does it track my searches?
**A:** No. Queries are encrypted POST requests that do not expose search terms in browser history. No account means no stored search history tied to your identity.

**Q:** Can I use it on mobile?
**A:** Yes, it is a responsive web app that works in any browser on desktop and mobile.

**Q:** How does Andi handle spam compared to Google?
**A:** Andi applies server-side spam blacklists before results are served and gives users tools to report and blacklist domains locally. Google relies heavily on automated ranking signals that spam farms game.

**Q:** What makes it different from other privacy search engines like DuckDuckGo?
**A:** DuckDuckGo sits in front of Bing and still returns traditional link lists. Andi generates synthesized AI answers with a conversational interface, similar to Perplexity but without the paywall or login gate.
