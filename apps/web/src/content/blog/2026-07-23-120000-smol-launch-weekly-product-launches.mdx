## What Is Smol Launch?

Smol Launch is a community launch platform built specifically for indie makers. Products are submitted every Monday and stay live through Sunday, giving each launch a full week in front of an audience of over 2,500 makers rather than the typical 24-hour Hacker News window.

The platform uses a multi-factor scoring algorithm to rank submissions — combining community votes with other signals — so early votes matter but are not the only factor. The free tier is genuinely free: basic submissions cap at 10 per week, and badge-verified launches are also free. A "Premium" tier and yearly "Maker Pass" skip the badge verification requirement and weekly submission cap. Paid "Featured Spots" are listed separately as advertising.

## How It Works

### Submitting a Product

Any maker can submit their product during the weekly submission window. There is no approval gate — submissions go live immediately and stay visible through Sunday.

### The Voting Window

Once live, submissions collect votes from the community through the end of Sunday. The scoring algorithm weighs multiple factors, not just raw vote count, which means a product with fewer votes can still rank well if other signals are strong.

### Free Tier Details

The free tier is designed to be genuinely free forever:

- **Basic Free** — capped at 10 submissions per week. No badge required.
- **Free Verified** — earn site authority by verifying the Smol Launch badge on your product. Badge-verified launches are free permanently.
- **Premium / Maker Pass** — removes the weekly cap and skips badge work. Launch Everywhere is a one-time $99 service to cross-post across the directory network.

## Key Features

- **Weekly cadence** — each launch runs Monday through Sunday, giving more time for feedback than a 24-hour HN window.
- **Multi-factor ranking** — algorithm combines votes with other signals to rank products, reducing the "first-mover wins" effect.
- **Badge verification system** — free Verified tier builds site authority without paying.
- **Launch Everywhere** — $99 one-time cross-posting across the Smol Launch directory network.
- **Featured Spots** — paid advertising placements separate from organic ranking.
