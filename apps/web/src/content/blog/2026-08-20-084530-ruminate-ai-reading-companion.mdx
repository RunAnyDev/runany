## What Is Ruminate?

Ruminate is an AI-powered reading companion designed to help users understand complex documents. You upload a PDF and the AI analyzes it block-by-block, providing explanations as you read. The system adapts to your stated learning objectives, making it particularly useful for dense technical papers, research articles, or any document with a high information density.

The product targets anyone who reads academic papers, technical documentation, or long-form content and wants a guided, interactive reading experience rather than a static document viewer.

## Key Features

Based on the product page and HN launch:

- **Block-by-block analysis** — the AI processes the document in segments rather than summarizing the whole thing at once
- **Interactive annotations** — highlight specific passages and receive targeted explanations
- **Context-aware chat** — ask questions about specific sections and get answers grounded in the document
- **LaTeX support** — renders and explains mathematical notation in technical papers
- **Learning objectives** — set a stated goal for each document to guide the AI's focus

## Setup Workflow

Ruminate runs entirely in the browser as a web application. There is no self-hosted option and no installable binary mentioned on the product page.

**Step 1: Open the web app**

Visit [tryruminate.com](https://tryruminate.com) and create an account if required.

**Step 2: Upload a PDF**

Click the upload interface and select any PDF from your device. The document is processed server-side and made available to the AI for analysis.

**Step 3: Set your learning objective**

Before reading, define what you want to get from the document. This shapes how the AI explains each section.

**Step 4: Read block-by-block**

Navigate through the document. Each block triggers analysis, annotation prompts, and explanation overlays generated by the AI.

**Step 5: Chat about specific sections**

Highlight any passage to open a context-aware chat. The AI answers based only on the surrounding document content.
