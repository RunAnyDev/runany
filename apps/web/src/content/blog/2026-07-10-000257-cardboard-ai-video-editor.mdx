## What Is Cardboard?

Cardboard is an agentic AI video editor that runs entirely in your browser. According to the product's own description, it "cuts, captions, reframes, and composes" video from a text prompt. There are no installs or downloads — you open the site, import your footage, and describe the edit you want.

The core pitch from their landing page:

> "An agentic AI video editor in your browser. Describe your edit and Cardboard cuts, captions, reframes, and composes it. No installs, no downloads."

Cardboard was part of Y Combinator's Winter 2026 cohort (YC W26).

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Create a Free Account

1. Go to [usecardboard.com](https://www.usecardboard.com)
2. Click **Get started** (top-right button)
3. Sign up with email or Google account
4. No credit card required for the free tier

### Step 2: Import Your Media

1. Click the import area or drag-and-drop your video files
2. Supported format: MOV, MP4, and common web formats
3. Files are uploaded to Cardboard's cloud storage (50 GB on Creator plan)

### Step 3: Describe Your Edit

1. The AI director shows a prompt: "What do you want to make today?"
2. Type your instruction in plain English, for example:
   - "Cut out the awkward pause at 0:32"
   - "Add captions to the entire video"
   - "Reframe this for vertical 9:16 format"
3. Cardboard processes the footage and returns the edited result

### Step 4: Export

1. Click **Reviewing footage** to preview the AI edit
2. If满意, export at your chosen resolution (up to 4K 60fps on Pro)
3. Export formats include MP4 with captions and reframed framing

## Pricing

All plans include built-in weekly and monthly usage limits. Extra credits start at $10, with bulk discounts up to 30% off. Credits never expire.

Key differences between plans:

- **Pro** — 10GB/file ingestion cap, 250GB cloud storage, 6× higher usage limits, faster exports, AI voiceovers, translation, animated captions, voice cloning, 4K 60fps exports
- **Teams** — Shared workspaces, centralized billing, per-seat pricing with Pro usage per member

## Key Features

### AI Editing Operations

From the product page and pricing page, Cardboard performs these operations via text prompt:

- **Cut** — removes silences, gaps, and unwanted segments
- **Captions** — auto-generates transcription and captions (including animated captions on Pro)
- **Reframe** — reformats footage between aspect ratios (e.g., horizontal to vertical)
- **Compose** — assembles multiple clips into a final sequence
- **AI voiceovers** — generates voiceover audio (Pro tier)
- **Translation** — translates audio/captions (Pro tier)
- **Stock B-roll** — access to stock video library (Pro tier)
- **Voice cloning** — clone a voice for consistent narration (Pro tier)

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to install anything to use Cardboard?**
**A:** No. Cardboard runs entirely in your browser. You just open usecardboard.com, sign up, and start editing.

**Q: What video formats does Cardboard support?**
**A:** Common formats including MOV and MP4. The Pro plan raises the per-file ingestion cap to 10GB.

**Q: Can I edit videos on my phone?**
**A:** Yes, since it runs in a browser, it works on mobile devices. However, serious editing work is more comfortable on a desktop.

**Q: Is there a free plan?**
**A:** Cardboard does not advertise a free tier. The Creator plan at $32/month is the entry-level option. Extra credits can be purchased without a subscription.

**Q: How does Cardboard compare to traditional video editors?**
**A:** Unlike Premiere Pro or Final Cut, you describe the edit in plain language rather than manually cutting clips, adding text overlays, and adjusting framing. It is best for creators who want finished video without learning editing software.

**Q: What happens to my uploaded videos?**
**A:** Videos are stored in Cardboard's cloud storage. Check the privacy policy for data retention and deletion terms.
