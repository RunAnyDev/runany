## What Is VideoToPage?

VideoToPage is a web-based tool that transforms video and audio content into written formats — blog posts, articles, and social media posts. The workflow is straightforward: paste a YouTube URL, upload a video file, or record directly in the browser. VideoToPage then processes the audio and outputs structured, readable content.

The idea came from a practical problem: recording a product demo creates a great walkthrough, but the content is locked inside a video. VideoToPage extracts that value and repackages it as text.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Input Your Video

Visit [videotopage.com](https://www.videotopage.com) and choose your input method:

- **YouTube URL** — paste any public YouTube link
- **File upload** — drop an MP4, MOV, or audio file
- **Record** — capture directly from your browser

### Step 2: AI Processing

The tool transcribes and analyzes the audio, identifying key topics, steps, and highlights. It structures the output as a coherent article or post rather than a raw transcript.

### Step 3: Export Your Content

Get a formatted blog post, article, or social media copy. You can edit and refine before publishing.
