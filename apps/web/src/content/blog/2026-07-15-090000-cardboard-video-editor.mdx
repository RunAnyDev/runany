## What Is Cardboard?

Cardboard is an agentic video editor that runs entirely in your browser. Instead of learning Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, you describe what you want in plain text and the AI handles the edit — cutting clips, adding captions, reframing for vertical/square/landscape formats, and composing multi-shot sequences.

From the Y Combinator company page:

> "We are building Cardboard — it is an agentic video editor that lives in your browser. Ask for a first cut ('make a 60s recap' / 'cut this into ads'). Search footage by meaning, not filenames. Then refine it yourself on a timeline."

Cardboard was part of Y Combinator's Winter 2026 batch (W26).

## Key Features

**Text-to-edit** — Describe the cut you want and Cardboard executes it. Examples from the product page include "make a 60s recap" or "cut this into ads."

**Semantic footage search** — Find the right clip by describing what's in it ("find quotes, reactions, people, emotions, and scenes across hours of footage") rather than scrubbing through timelines or guessing at filenames.

**Automatic reformatting** — Turn one video into vertical, square, and landscape versions simultaneously. Cardboard keeps the important action in frame across all formats.

**AI-directed reframing** — Snap at the line of scrimmage kicks off the play. Running back breaks through for a big gain. Cardboard tracks action automatically.

**Real-time collaboration** — Share a project, leave comments at exact timecodes, and work alongside your team in the same session. No exports, no version sprawl.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Open the Web App

No install required. Navigate to [usecardboard.com](https://www.usecardboard.com/) and sign up.

```bash
open https://www.usecardboard.com/
```

### Step 2: Upload Your Footage

Drag and drop video files into the browser window. Cardboard processes footage server-side and indexes it for semantic search.

### Step 3: Make Your First Cut

Type a prompt describing the edit you want. For example:

- "Make a 60-second recap of our product launch event"
- "Cut this footage into three 15-second ads for social media"
- "Find all the moments where someone is laughing"

Cardboard generates the edit and presents it on a timeline for your review.

### Step 4: Export or Share

When you're happy with the cut, export in your desired format or share the project link with collaborators for inline feedback at specific timecodes.

If you're evaluating Cardboard for your team, here is what to verify:

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need a powerful computer to run Cardboard?**
**A:** No — the editing engine runs on Cardboard's servers, not your local machine. You need a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and a stable internet connection. Heavy lifting is offloaded to the cloud.

**Q: Can I export to professional formats like ProRes or DNxHD?**
**A:** Check the export options on your plan. Consumer formats (MP4, WebM) are likely supported; professional intermediate codecs may require a higher tier.

**Q: How does Cardboard handle footage with multiple people talking?**
**A:** From the product description, Cardboard can identify "quotes, reactions, people, emotions, and scenes." For detailed speaker separation or transcription, a dedicated tool like Descript may complement it.
