## What Is AllPub?

Every content creator faces the same headache: write a blog post, then manually reformat and copy-paste it to Medium, Dev.to, Hashnode, your own site, maybe a newsletter. Each platform has its own quirks — headings, code blocks, images, embedding. Doing it by hand is error-prone and time-consuming.

AllPub solves this with a "write once, publish everywhere" workflow. You compose in AllPub using a structured editor, and it handles the formatting translation to each target platform automatically.

The pitch from the HN announcement was straightforward: save hours of manual cross-posting and reach your entire audience across all platforms without the repetitive work.

## Setup Workflow

AllPub is a web-based tool — no installation required.

### Step 1: Create an Account

Visit https://www.allpub.co and sign up with your email or a supported OAuth provider.

### Step 2: Compose Your Content

Use the AllPub editor to write your article. The editor supports:

- Rich text with headings, bold, italic, links
- Code blocks with syntax highlighting
- Image embedding
- Tables and blockquotes

### Step 3: Connect Your Publishing Platforms

Link your accounts for each target platform:

- Medium
- Dev.to
- Hashnode
- Ghost
- Substack
- Custom webhooks for your own site

### Step 4: Publish with One Click

When your content is ready, select your target platforms and hit Publish. AllPub handles the format conversion for each platform's specific requirements.

## Deeper Analysis

### What Makes It Different

Most cross-posting tools either use RSS feeds or require you to paste content into their UI. AllPub takes a more structured approach — content is stored as structured data (not just HTML), and the publishing layer handles platform-specific transformation.

This means if Medium doesn't support a specific formatting feature, AllPub degrades gracefully rather than mangling your layout.

### Supported Platforms

Based on the site and HN discussion, AllPub supports:

- Medium
- Dev.to
- Hashnode
- Ghost
- Substack
- Custom webhook (for headless CMS integration)

### Content Deduplication

Platforms like Medium and Dev.to may flag or penalize duplicate content. AllPub generates platform-native content rather than scraping HTML, which helps avoid content-scraping detection from search engines.
