## What Is LiftmyCV?

LiftmyCV is a web-based AI agent designed to automate the tedious parts of job hunting. Instead of manually applying to each posting, you configure your profile once and let the AI handle repetitive tasks:

- **Job matching** - AI finds relevant openings based on your resume and preferences
- **Auto-apply** - Submits applications across 12 major ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) and job boards
- **Tailored documents** - Generates custom resumes and cover letters per application
- **Pipeline tracking** - Dashboard shows where each application stands

It positions itself as a time-saver for active job seekers tired of reformatting resumes for every role.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Create Your Profile

Sign up at [liftmycv.com](https://www.liftmycv.com) and import your existing resume. The importer accepts PDF and Word formats.

### Step 2: Connect Job Boards

Link your accounts across the supported platforms. Currently covers major ATS providers used by enterprise recruiters.

### Step 3: Define Preferences

Set target roles, salary ranges, locations, and visa sponsorship requirements. The AI uses these to filter opportunities before applying.

### Step 4: Launch the Agent

Toggle auto-apply on. The agent runs in the background, submitting to matched positions and generating custom application materials.

## Deeper Analysis

### Where It Fits in the Workflow

LiftmyCV targets the volume game of job searching. For each matched role, it:

1. Scrapes the job description for keywords
2. Rewrites resume bullets to match the job description
3. Generates a cover letter with role-specific framing
4. Submits credentials to the ATS

The value prop is clear for passive candidates or those applying to many similar roles. The risk is low effort applications triggering rejection algorithms, though the tailored documents mitigate this somewhat.

### Target User

- Software engineers and product managers mass-applying to reduce time-to-interview
- Career switchers needing high application volume to break into new fields
- International candidates navigating visa requirements across multiple employers

### Pricing

Free tier available with limited applications per month. Premium tiers unlock higher volume and advanced customization.
