## What Is Indie Launch?

Indie Launch is a web-based tool for indie developers and solo founders who want a concrete launch strategy without spending weeks researching channels. You answer four questions about your product, audience, and goals, and Indie Launch returns a personalized plan: which platforms to post on, in what order, and with pre-written content ready to copy-paste.

The core claim on the site:

> "Get a personalized launch plan for your indie product. The right channels, ready-made content, and a step-by-step guide to get your first users. $10 one-time payment."

Key features as listed on the product page:

- Personalized launch plan matched to your product and audience
- Channel matching across 70+ platforms
- Ready-made content for each platform
- Platform guides and best practices
- Post-launch growth strategy
- Up to 5 projects per account
- No subscription — one-time $10 payment

## How It Works

### Step 1: Answer 4 Questions

The intake form asks about your product type, target audience, whether you have an existing audience, and your launch timeline. This takes under a minute.

### Step 2: Receive Your Launch Plan

Indie Launch generates a sequenced plan across three phases:

**Day 1 — Show HN (recommended as primary):**
The site cites analysis of 387 indie launches finding that Indie Hackers converts 23× better than Product Hunt for developer tools with no existing audience. For developer tools specifically, it recommends starting with a Show HN post. The generated post is formatted as "Show HN: I built a launch tracker that tells indie devs which platform to post on first" — framed as a problem-solving tool rather than a product announcement.

**Day 1–2 — Reddit communities:**
Suggested posts for r/SideProject and r/startups, with timing guidance (Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10 AM EST, avoiding weekends).

**Week 1 — Indie Hackers:**
A full launch post optimized for the Indie Hackers community format, with notes on what resonates there vs. HN.

### Step 3: Channel Coverage Map

The product lists 70+ channels organized by type:

- **Communities:** Hacker News, Reddit (r/SideProject, r/startups), Indie Hackers, Dev.to
- **Social:** Twitter/X, LinkedIn
- **Directories:** Product Hunt, BetaList, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, Futurepedia
- **Content:** Medium, Starter Story, YouTube, StackShare

The tool matches your product to the most relevant subset rather than dumping all 70+ options.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the $10 payment a subscription?**
**A:** No. It is a one-time payment with lifetime access. The site explicitly states "No subscription, ever."

**Q: Does it work for non-developer products?**
**A:** The channel recommendations are skewed toward developer-adjacent platforms (HN, Indie Hackers, Dev.to). For consumer products, the Reddit and social channels would be more relevant, but the core analysis appears focused on dev-tool launches.

**Q: How many projects can I use it for?**
**A:** Up to 5 projects per account.

**Q: What if the generated content does not fit my product?**
**A:** The site shows a "Regenerate" option per platform, suggesting the content is AI-generated and can be re-rolled.

**Q: Does it have a free trial?**
**A:** No free trial is visible on the site. The $10 payment is required to access the full plan.

## Conclusion

Indie Launch solves the "where do I even post this?" paralysis that hits most first-time indie hackers. At $10, it is cheaper than a lunch and more actionable than a generic "launch on PH and HN" advice list. The sequenced Day 1 / Day 2 / Week 1 structure gives new founders a concrete schedule instead of an overwhelming checklist.

The main limitation is that it is optimized for dev-tool and SaaS launches. If your audience is non-technical consumers, the channel recommendations will be less targeted. But for developer-adjacent indie products, the HN-first sequencing and Indie Hackers data point make it a credible shortcut.

If you are preparing to launch your first indie product and do not have an existing audience, this is worth the $10.
