## What Is TeamOut?

TeamOut is an AI-powered event planning agent built specifically for corporate retreats and offsites. The core idea mirrors how Lovable helps you build websites through chat — instead of filling out forms, navigating complex booking interfaces, or sending dozens of emails, you describe what you want and the agent handles the rest.

The agent works across the full retreat planning lifecycle:

- **Venue sourcing** — finding and comparing retreat locations based on your requirements
- **Vendor coordination** — communicating with hotels, activity providers, and caterers
- **Flight cost estimation** — getting realistic travel cost projections for your team size
- **Itinerary building** — constructing day-by-day schedules that account for logistics
- **Overall project management** — tracking decisions, deadlines, and dependencies

## How It Works

### Conversational Interface

You interact with TeamOut through a chat interface, similar to how you would work with a human event planner. Instead of navigating a traditional SaaS dashboard with forms and filters, you describe your needs in plain language:

```text
You: We need a venue for 20 people in the Bay Area, end of September,
     budget around $15k total. Prefer somewhere with outdoor activities.

TeamOut: Great, I found three options that match your criteria:
         [Oakland hills retreat] [Mill Valley conference center] [Napa estate]
         
         Here's a quick comparison of availability and pricing for your dates...
```

### Venue Sourcing and Comparison

TeamOut maintains relationships with venue partners and can pull availability data on demand. When you describe your requirements (location, headcount, dates, budget, preferred amenities), it returns a ranked shortlist with key details — not a raw list of 200 options to filter yourself.

### Vendor Coordination

Once a venue is selected, TeamOut can initiate and manage communication with vendors. This includes:
- Sending RFPs (Request for Proposals) to multiple venues simultaneously
- Following up on pending responses
- Collecting and comparing quotes in a structured format
- Tracking contract status and key deadlines

### Cost Estimation

Corporate retreat costs escalate quickly — not just the venue, but flights, ground transportation, meals, activities, and incidentals. TeamOut maintains a cost model that provides realistic estimates based on your headcount, destination, and trip duration. This helps budget owners avoid the common surprise of "why did this cost 3x what we expected?"

### Itinerary Building

The itinerary feature constructs a schedule from your stated preferences — timing of sessions vs. downtime, preferred activity types, dietary requirements, and any mandatory meetings. The output is a structured agenda that can be shared with attendees or imported into calendar tools.

## Practical Evaluation Checklist

When evaluating TeamOut for your next company retreat:

- Does the conversational interface handle complex, multi-constraint queries well?
- How current is the venue database — does it include newer properties, not just chains?
- Is the cost estimation model accurate enough to use for budget planning, or just directional?
- Can the itinerary be exported to Google Calendar, Notion, or other tools your team uses?
- What happens when a vendor doesn't respond or a venue becomes unavailable mid-planning?

## Security Notes

TeamOut processes corporate planning data — headcount, budget, travel dates, and team member names. If your company has data residency requirements or strict vendor vetting policies, confirm with TeamOut how data is stored and whether vendor communications are managed on your behalf or stored in their systems.

The platform uses standard HTTPS for all communications. No agent installation is required — it runs as a web application.

## FAQ

**Q:** How does TeamOut handle complex multi-location retreats (e.g., a three-city roadshow)?

**A:** The conversational interface can handle multi-destination trips, but the primary use case is single-location retreats. For roadshows, the cost estimation and logistics complexity increases significantly — confirm with TeamOut whether your specific scenario is supported before committing.

**Q:** What integrations are available?

**A:** The product offers a YouTube demo and web interface. Calendar integration and export functionality are mentioned in the documentation, but the specific tools (Google Calendar, Notion, etc.) should be confirmed directly with TeamOut.

**Q:** Is there a free tier or trial?

**A:** The product is in public beta. Check teamout.com for current pricing and trial availability.

**Q:** How does TeamOut compare to using a human event planner?

**A:** TeamOut is faster for initial research and comparison (venue shortlisting, cost estimates). A human planner adds value for complex negotiations, relationship-based upgrades, and handling edge cases — especially for high-stakes executive offsites where the cost of a bad venue is significant.

## Conclusion

TeamOut targets a real pain point: planning a company retreat is time-consuming and requires juggling many vendors simultaneously, but most teams only do it a few times a year — not often enough to build expertise. The AI agent approach is well-suited to this because the core tasks (venue search, quote comparison, itinerary construction) are time-intensive but follow a clear logic that software can automate.

The conversational interface is the right UX choice for this domain — corporate retreat planning involves many subjective preferences (vibe, "not too corporate", "good wifi but not too sterile") that are easier to express in chat than in a filter form.

If your company runs regular offsites and you're tired of spending a day researching venues only to discover they don't have availability, TeamOut is worth a try.
