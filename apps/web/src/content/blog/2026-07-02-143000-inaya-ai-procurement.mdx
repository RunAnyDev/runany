## What Is INAYA?

INAYA is an AI-powered procurement intelligence platform built for commodity-exposed teams in manufacturing, CPG, and industrials. The founding team is based in Italy and launched the product in 2024.

The platform sits between a company's ERP/Procurement system and the commodity markets, giving procurement leads, finance teams, and CFOs a shared view of forward exposure — and a defensible record of every major buying decision.

The core thesis on the site: procurement teams have market data, and they have decisions, but the **layer connecting the two** — the ability to know what a decision will cost before you make it, and prove why you made it afterward — is missing.

Four integrated capabilities make up the platform:

**Market Foresight** delivers bull/base/bear commodity scenarios on a 24-month horizon, updated continuously. Each forecast carries a probability weighting and cites the macro drivers behind the call. The claim is over 90% directional accuracy on 1–24 month horizons.

**Product Composer** lets teams build live cost models by linking every BOM line to a commodity index or supplier quote. FX adjustment is built in, and the model recalculates forward unit cost automatically when a commodity price moves.

**Decision Governance** is an immutable audit trail attached to every fixing decision. Each record captures the scenario alternatives considered, the model version used, who approved it, and the market conditions at decision time — producing a PDF summary committee-ready on request.

**Lisa** is the conversational interface: a chat layer inside Teams, Slack, and Email where procurement staff can ask commodity exposure questions in plain language and get answers backed by the same forecasting models powering the platform.

## Setup Workflow

INAYA is a fully hosted SaaS product. There is no self-hosted deployment option and no developer install required to get started.

### Step 1: Request Access

Visit [inaya.ai](https://inaya.ai) and click **"Get started"** to book a 30-minute working session with the founding team, or request a 30-day full-feature trial directly.

The trial requires no IT integration and no contract. Setup time to a first defended decision is quoted at seven days.

### Step 2: Connect Your Data Sources

During onboarding, INAYA connects to your existing procurement and ERP data. The specific connector list was not enumerated on the public site, but the platform supports integration with Procore and QuickBooks based on the feature schema.

Key data inputs include:

- Open purchase orders and supplier contracts (exposure quantification)
- Commodity indices referenced in supplier agreements (LME 3M aluminum, ICIS PP, HRC US steel, etc.)
- FX rates for internationally sourced materials

### Step 3: Define Your Commodity Exposure

Set up your commodity universe — the specific indices and SKUs your business is exposed to. INAYA supports modeling across the most widely referenced commodity benchmarks including metals (aluminum, steel), polymers (polypropylene), energy (EUR-IT kWh), and freight (DAT-EU km).

### Step 4: Run Your First Forecast

Once configured, Market Foresight generates a 24-month bull/base/bear scenario fan for each commodity in your universe. Each scenario includes:

- Price path trajectory
- Probability weighting (base case typically p=0.74 in example outputs)
- Macro narrative explaining the key drivers

You can query any commodity in plain language via Lisa (in Teams or Slack) and get a scenario-backed answer in seconds.
