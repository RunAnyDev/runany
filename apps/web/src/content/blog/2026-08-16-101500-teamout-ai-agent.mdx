## What Is TeamOut?

TeamOut is a web-based AI agent that handles the entire workflow of planning a company retreat. Instead of juggling spreadsheets, email threads, and venue websites, you describe what you want — destination, budget, team size, dates, vibe — and TeamOut returns a structured proposal with venue shortlists, accommodation options, activity ideas, and a draft itinerary.

The product sits at the intersection of travel planning and enterprise logistics. It is primarily aimed at HR teams, ops managers, and founders who need to coordinate offsite events without engaging a traditional events agency.

## Setup Workflow

TeamOut runs entirely in the browser. There is no self-hosted component, no CLI, and no local setup required.

### Step 1: Open the App

Navigate to [app.teamout.com/ai](https://app.teamout.com/ai). No sign-up is required to start a planning session, though an account is needed to save and share itineraries.

### Step 2: Describe Your Retreat

You start by filling in a short form:

- **Destination** — city or region
- **Dates** — approximate or fixed
- **Team size** — number of attendees
- **Budget range** — approximate per-person or total
- **Vibe** — inputs like "relaxed", "team-building focused", "adventure", or "offsite with some workshops"

### Step 3: Review the AI-Generated Plan

TeamOut produces a structured proposal that typically includes:

- **Venue options** — hotels and event spaces with capacity and price estimates
- **Activities** — curated ideas based on the destination and group profile
- **Draft itinerary** — a day-by-day schedule with time blocks
- **Logistics notes** — transport considerations, dietary requirements handling

You can iterate on any section by asking follow-up questions in natural language, for example: "make it more kid-friendly" or "cut the budget in half."

## Deeper Analysis

### What makes it different from a travel agent?

A traditional travel agent handles flights and accommodation but typically does not manage the internal logistics of a company offsite — team activities, agenda scheduling, and internal communication fall outside their scope. TeamOut's agent is built specifically for the corporate retreat context, which means it reasons about group dynamics, activity clustering, and agenda flow in ways a standard travel tool does not.

### What it does not cover

TeamOut does not book flights or handle visa arrangements. It produces recommendations and draft plans — the user is still responsible for final bookings and internal alignment.

### Pricing

TeamOut operates on a per-retreat credit model. The specific credit pricing and plan tiers are available on the TeamOut website. A free tier with limited generations appears to exist for evaluation purposes, though the exact limits are set by the vendor.

## Practical Evaluation Checklist

- Destination coverage: city-level, not limited to major hubs
- Iteration model: can refine any section of the plan via follow-up prompts
- Output format: structured itinerary vs. prose paragraphs
- Sharing: can exported plans be shared with stakeholders who do not have an account
- Integration: any calendar or project management tool integrations

## Security Notes

- TeamOut is a hosted SaaS product. Retreat data (team size, dates, budget, internal notes) is stored on TeamOut's servers.
- Review their privacy policy and data handling terms before uploading sensitive org charts or internal planning documents.
- No self-hosted deployment option is available at this time.

**A:** No. TeamOut generates recommendations and draft itineraries. You handle the actual bookings through your own channels.

## Conclusion

TeamOut fills a specific gap in the corporate retreat planning workflow: the back-and-forth between intent and a concrete plan.
