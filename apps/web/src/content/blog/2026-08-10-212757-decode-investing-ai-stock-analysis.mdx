## What Is Decode Investing?

Decode Investing is a web-based investment research platform that uses AI to help retail investors discover and analyze stock market opportunities. Rather than browsing dozens of financial sites, users can ask plain-English questions like "What tech companies have strong free cash flow and are trading below intrinsic value?" and receive a synthesized answer drawing from public financial data.

The platform positions itself as a democratizing force in investment research — making analysis that was previously only available to institutional investors with Bloomberg terminals and analyst teams accessible to anyone with a browser.

### Core Capabilities

Based on the product description and feature set, Decode Investing covers several key areas of investment research:

**Natural Language Querying** — Users type investment questions in plain English and receive answers synthesized from publicly available financial data. The AI interprets the intent behind the question and retrieves relevant information from SEC filings, earnings reports, news articles, and market data.

**Multi-Source Research Synthesis** — Rather than presenting raw data, the platform aggregates information across multiple public sources and presents it in a coherent narrative. This includes fundamental analysis (earnings, revenue, cash flow), technical indicators (price trends, moving averages), and macro themes (sector performance, interest rate sensitivity).

**Sector and Theme Analysis** — The platform can analyze entire sectors or investment themes, identifying common risk factors, competitive dynamics, and valuation disparities across companies within the same industry group.

**Free Access Model** — The platform is free to use, removing the cost barrier that typically separates retail investors from professional-grade research tools.
