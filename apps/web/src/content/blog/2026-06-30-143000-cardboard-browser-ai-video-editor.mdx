## What Is Cardboard?

Cardboard is a browser-based AI video editor built by [Cardboard Inc.](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cardboard) in San Francisco, part of Y Combinator's Winter 2026 batch. The core pitch: you describe the edit you want in plain text, and Cardboard's AI handles the cutting, captioning, reframing, and compositing.

The product targets creators who want professional edits without learning Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve. From the launch thread, one early user described it as "the kind of tool I was thinking I wish existed" for sending personal videos to a loved one.

Key capabilities as described on the site:

- **Plain-text editing** — "cut the beginning", "add captions", "reframe for vertical" in natural language
- **Caption generation** — auto-generated subtitles
- **Reframing** — adapts horizontal footage to vertical for TikTok/Reels/Shorts
- **Compositing** — multi-clip composition without a timeline
- **No install** — runs in the browser, nothing to download

## Setup Workflow

Cardboard requires no local installation. Getting started:

### Step 1: Sign Up

Visit [usecardboard.com](https://usecardboard.com) and create an account. The pricing page shows Free and paid tiers (annual plans get a discount).

### Step 2: Upload Your Footage

Drag and drop video files directly into the browser window. Cardboard processes files server-side.

### Step 3: Describe Your Edit

Type what you want in plain text. Example edit commands:

```text
"Cut the first 10 seconds and add captions"
"Reframe this horizontal video for Instagram Reels"
"Trim the awkward pause at 1:23 and add a fade"
```

The AI interprets the instruction and applies the edit automatically.

Most AI video editors (Runway, CapCut, Descript) require either a desktop install or a heavy web app. Cardboard's fully browser-based approach eliminates the install friction entirely — useful for quick edits on borrowed or managed machines.
