## What Is horch?

horch is a native macOS app that acts as a **meeting memory** — it records your meetings, transcribes them on-device using local speech recognition, and automatically extracts:

- **Todos** — action items assigned during the meeting
- **Topics** — the subject areas discussed
- **People pages** — a profile per participant with their contributions

The core promise is in the tagline: *"Your first brain is for thinking; this one does the remembering."*

### Key Features

- **On-device transcription** — zero bytes uploaded, all processing happens locally on your Mac
- **Automatic extraction** — todos, topics, and people surfaces are generated automatically from the transcript
- **Plain Markdown output** — meeting summaries saved as Markdown files in a folder you control
- **No account required** — no cloud signup, no subscription for core functionality
- **Native macOS** — built for Apple Silicon, runs as a local app
- **Free trial** — 30-day trial, no account needed

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Download and Install

Download the latest `.dmg` from the horch website:

```
https://horch.app
```

Mount the `.dmg` and drag horch to your Applications folder. Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon.

### Step 2: Start a Meeting

Open horch and start a meeting recording. horch sits in your menu bar and begins transcribing when you activate it.

### Step 3: Review Generated Memory

After the meeting, horch generates three surfaces:

- **Todos** — action items surfaced from the transcript
- **Topics** — subject areas covered, with summaries
- **People** — a per-participant page with their key contributions

All outputs are plain Markdown in a folder you choose.

### Step 4: Configure Storage (Optional)

By default, horch saves meeting summaries as plain Markdown to a folder you specify. This is fully under your control — no proprietary format lock-in.

## FAQ

**Q: Does horch work on Intel Macs?**
**A:** No. horch requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later.

**Q: What happens after the free trial ends?**
**A:** The trial runs for 30 days. After that, you purchase a license (€49 once) which includes a year of updates. After the first year, horch continues working at the version you have — renewal at €25 per year is optional for new updates only.

**Q: Can I use horch for video calls (Zoom, Meet, etc.)?**
**A:** horch records from your Mac's microphone. For video calls, you would run horch alongside your call app so it captures the audio output through your speakers.

**Q: Where are the meeting summaries stored?**
**A:** As plain Markdown files in a folder you choose on your Mac. No proprietary format lock-in.

**Q: Is there a subscription?**
**A:** No subscription for the core license. One-time purchase of €49, with optional €25/year renewal for updates.
