## TL;DR

> **TL;DR:** Superpowered captures meeting audio at the OS level to generate AI-powered notes, action items, and summaries — no bot needs to join the call.

## What Is Superpowered?

Most AI meeting tools work by having a robot join your call. Superpowered takes a different approach: it captures audio directly from your operating system's audio stream, so the meeting platform sees nothing but your voice.

From the product page:

> "Superpowered is the AI notetaker for your meetings. It transcribes your meetings without using bots and gives you great notes after."

The product sits in your menu bar (macOS) or system tray (Windows) and integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, and Around. After each meeting, you get structured AI-generated notes including a summary, key points, and action items.

The company is backed by Y Combinator (W21 batch) and has been covered by TechCrunch.

## Setup

### Step 1: Download and Install

Download from [superpowered.me/download](https://superpowered.me/download). The app is available for macOS and Windows.

### Step 2: Connect Your Calendar

On first launch, Superpowered prompts you to connect your calendar (Google Calendar or Outlook). This enables automatic meeting detection and one-click join links.

### Step 3: Grant Audio Access

Superpowered needs permission to capture system audio. On macOS, this requires granting the app screen recording or audio capture permission in System Preferences > Security & Privacy. Windows handles this through standard app permission dialogs.

### Step 4: Join a Meeting Normally

Once configured, you join meetings as usual — Superpowered captures the audio in the background without any bot joining the call. Notes appear in the Superpowered app and sync to your dashboard after the meeting ends.

## How It Differs From Alternatives

Tools like Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, and Gong all work by having an AI bot join your meeting as a participant. Superpowered's OS-level audio capture means:

- No bot appears in the participant list
- Meeting organizers do not need to approve a third-party bot
- Audio is captured regardless of which meeting platform is used (for supported platforms)

The tradeoff is that OS-level capture requires the app to be running and have the appropriate permissions granted, whereas bot-based tools only need a shared meeting link.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Superpowered record both sides of the conversation?**
**A:** Yes, when you join a meeting while Superpowered is running, it captures all audio from the call — including other participants' voices — and transcribes the full meeting.

**Q: Does the meeting platform see Superpowered as a participant?**
**A:** No. Superpowered captures audio at the OS level, so it does not appear as a bot or guest in the meeting participant list.

**Q: What happens to my meeting data?**
**A:** Transcripts and AI-generated notes are stored in your Superpowered account. The security page references SOC-2 Type II compliance. Review their privacy policy for data retention specifics.

**Q: Is there a free trial for paid plans?**
**A:** The Free plan is permanently free. Paid plan trials are not explicitly advertised on the pricing page — check the site for current trial offers.
