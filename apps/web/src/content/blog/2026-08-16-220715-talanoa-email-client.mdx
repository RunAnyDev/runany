## What Is Talanoa?

Most email clients organize your inbox chronologically — newest on top. Talanoa inverts that model: **inbox organized by sender**, so you see all emails from one person or company grouped together. The goal is to reduce context-switching and help you track conversations as coherent threads rather than scattered time-ordered messages.

The product ships with a built-in **Kanban board** for turning emails into tasks. You can create cards from emails, assign status columns (like To Do, In Progress, Done), and manage your workflow without a separate task app.

From Talanoa's own feature list:

- People-first inbox organized by sender
- Built-in Kanban board for email tasks
- One-click email unsubscribe
- One-click sender blocking
- 100% local storage — email never leaves your device
- Zero tracking, zero telemetry
- Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, and IMAP support
- Multi-account inbox management

The security page claims **CASA Tier 2 certified** status, which is an independent security certification. The app never transmits your emails or OAuth tokens to Talanoa's servers.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Download

Talanoa supports **macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 10+, and Linux (Debian/Ubuntu .deb and Fedora/RHEL .rpm)**.

Head to [talanoa.email/download](https://talanoa.email/download) and pick your platform.

### Step 2: Connect an Email Account

On first launch, Talanoa walks you through account setup. Supported providers:

- Gmail / Google Workspace
- Microsoft Outlook / Hotmail
- Yahoo Mail
- iCloud Mail
- Any standard IMAP/SMTP server (including self-hosted)

The free **Focus Mode** plan supports one account. Paid **Power Mode** unlocks unlimited accounts.

### Step 3: People-First Inbox

Once connected, Talanoa automatically groups incoming emails by sender. All emails from the same address collapse into a single conversation thread. This means:

- You see the full history with a person or service at a glance
- New messages from the same sender stay together regardless of when they arrived
- Promotional and notification emails also get grouped, making them easier to triage or block

### Step 4: Kanban Task Board

From any email thread, you can create a Kanban card. Talanoa creates a default board with columns, or you can customize them. Cards link back to the original email thread, so context is always preserved.

## Security Notes

Talanoa is **CASA Tier 2 certified**, which independently verifies that:

- Emails and OAuth tokens are stored only on-device
- No data is transmitted to Talanoa's servers
- The app has zero telemetry or tracking

For teams handling sensitive communications, this local-first architecture means your email data never touches a third-party cloud. This is a meaningful distinction from Gmail/Outlook web clients or SaaS email tools that necessarily process your mail on their servers.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Talanoa actually open source?**
**A:** The website states "open-source" and links to a GitHub org at `github.com/talanoa-email`. However, no public repository or LICENSE file was found at publication time. If you find the source code, please share the link.

**Q: Does Talanoa work with my custom domain email?**
**A:** Yes, Talanoa supports any standard IMAP/SMTP server. You can connect a self-hosted mail server, custom domain provider, or any email service that exposes IMAP access.

**Q: Can I use Talanoa without an internet connection?**
**A:** Once your emails are synced to the local app, you can read and compose messages offline. Outbound messages queue and send when connectivity is restored.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
**A:** Not yet. Talanoa is currently desktop-only (macOS, Windows, Linux). A mobile app is listed on their public roadmap.

**Q: How is Talanoa different from Gmail's sender grouping?**
**A:** Gmail's sender grouping is a display-layer feature that doesn't change how emails are stored. Talanoa builds its entire inbox model around sender relationships, making it the primary organizational unit rather than a visual overlay on a chronological list.
