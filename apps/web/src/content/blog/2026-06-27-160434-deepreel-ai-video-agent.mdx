## What Is DeepReel?

DeepReel is a web-based AI video creation platform. Its headline feature, the "AI Video Genie," acts as an end-to-end video production team: drop in a blog URL, PDF, or topic, and it outputs a complete explainer video ready for YouTube, social media, or embedding on a website.

The tool targets content creators, marketers, and businesses that want to reuse written content as video — without hiring editors or learning video software.

DeepReel started as an AI avatar company in 2023. According to the founder's HN launch post, DeepReel was the first startup to automate custom avatar production — creating photorealistic AI avatars in 24 hours instead of the typical 10-day studio pipeline. The team has worked with ElevenLabs and the Aston Martin F1 team to create Fernando Alonso's AI avatar.

The pivot to full video generation came from observing users stringing together multiple AI tools (N8N workflows, caption generators, voiceover services) just to produce basic videos from written content.

## How DeepReel Works

### Step 1: Input Your Content

You can provide content in three ways:

- A public blog URL (DeepReel scrapes and analyses the article)
- A PDF document
- A plain text topic or prompt

### Step 2: AI Video Genie Produces the Video

DeepReel's AI agent handles the full pipeline:

1. **Script writing** — A fine-tuned LLM (trained on high-performing educational videos) breaks down the source material into scenes with engaging hooks and narrative flow, not just a plain summary
2. **AI presenter or voiceover** — Choose from 100+ photorealistic AI presenters or a text-to-speech voiceover in 30+ languages
3. **Visuals and B-roll** — Multi-modal embeddings match stock or AI-generated visuals to each scene's narration (semantic matching, not keyword search)
4. **Captions and music** — Auto-generated captions and royalty-free music are added automatically
5. **Editing decisions** — The agent controls pacing, transitions, effects, and animations to keep the video dynamic

### Step 3: Fine-Tune in the Editor

A web-based editor lets you tweak scenes, swap presenters, adjust the script, or swap out visuals. The founder notes that heavy lifting is done by the AI, but nothing is locked — you can override any decision.
