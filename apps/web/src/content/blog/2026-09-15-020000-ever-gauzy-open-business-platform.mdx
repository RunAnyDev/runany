> **TL;DR:** Ever Gauzy is an ambitious open-source business management platform that bundles ERP, CRM, HRM, ATS, and project management into a single TypeScript codebase. It supports multi-tenant SaaS deployment and is licensed under AGPL-3.0.

## What It Is

Ever Gauzy is an open-source platform that combines the operational functions of a small-to-medium business into one application:

- **ERP** for accounting, inventory, invoicing, and operations.
- **CRM** for sales pipelines, customer records, and contact management.
- **HRM** for employees, time tracking, payroll, and leave.
- **ATS** (applicant tracking system) for recruitment workflows.
- **Project management** for tasks, timesheets, and team coordination.

The codebase is TypeScript end-to-end. The platform supports both single-tenant self-hosted deployments and multi-tenant SaaS, which makes it usable both as an in-house back-office system and as the foundation for a vertical SaaS product.

The project has been under continuous development for years, with a sizable community and an active commercial entity behind it. The commercial arm offers hosted and supported versions for teams that don't want to run it themselves.

## Why It Matters

Most "open-source ERP" projects are either thin CRMs that grew aspirations or stale forks of old enterprise code. Ever Gauzy is interesting because it actually ships a coherent set of modules at the same time, all from one TypeScript codebase that follows modern web conventions.

For the right audience, that's a meaningful proposition:

- **Agencies and consultancies** that want one tool for time tracking, project billing, HR, and sales pipelines.
- **Small and medium businesses** that want to consolidate back-office tooling into a single, self-hostable platform.
- **Vertical SaaS builders** who want an open-source foundation to fork rather than building from scratch.
- **Cooperatives and non-profits** that need real ERP/HRM capabilities without SaaS pricing.

The TypeScript codebase is approachable to a typical full-stack web developer. That's a low barrier compared to the Java/Python stacks that dominate the open-source ERP space.

## Practical Trade-Offs

A few honest caveats:

- **AGPL-3.0.** Strong copyleft. Self-hosting for internal use is fine. If you build a hosted product on top of Ever Gauzy, the AGPL terms apply. Commercial licensing is available through the project's commercial arm.
- **Module depth varies.** The combined-platform pitch is real, but the depth of any single module may not match a best-of-breed tool. A dedicated HRM product will have more HR features; Ever Gauzy covers the common cases well and the edges less so.
- **Operational overhead.** A platform with this many modules has a meaningful setup footprint. Expect a real install procedure, not a one-line Docker.
- **Roadmap pace.** As an open-source project with commercial backing, some features land in the commercial product first. Check the issue tracker and roadmap before committing to self-hosting.
- **Customization cost.** Like any broad-scope platform, deep customization of any module takes real engineering time. The win of "one platform" can become "one platform to maintain."

These are normal caveats for a full-stack business management suite. The trade-off between breadth and depth is the central design tension, not a bug.

## Who Should Care

Ever Gauzy is a strong fit for:

- **Agencies** that need time tracking, project billing, and HRM in one place.
- **Service businesses** with a mix of project work, sales pipeline, and back-office accounting.
- **Multi-location SMBs** that need centralized employee and operations management.
- **Vertical SaaS builders** who want an open-source ERP/CRM foundation.
- **Cooperatives and non-profits** that want modern, web-based business management without SaaS subscription costs.

If you only need CRM and are happy with a focused tool like EspoCRM or Twenty, Ever Gauzy may be more platform than you want. If you need many of those modules at once, the consolidation is a real win.

## Summary

Ever Gauzy is one of the more ambitious open-source business management platforms shipping today. The TypeScript codebase, multi-tenant architecture, and integrated ERP/CRM/HRM/ATS/PM scope make it a credible alternative to either a fragmented SaaS stack or a heavy traditional ERP. The AGPL license shapes the commercial path, but for self-hosters and verticals, it's a substantive foundation.
