## What Is Cardboard?

Video editing traditionally requires either a steep learning curve (Premiere, DaVinci) or expensive editors (Final Cut). Cardboard flips this by putting an agentic AI editor directly in your browser — no install, no subscription, no export loops.

You describe what you want in plain text. The agent then:
- Cuts clips to match your description
- Adds captions automatically
- Reframes shots for better composition
- Composes multiple clips into a final output

Under the hood, it uses browser-based video processing with AI models handling the scene understanding, cut decisions, and caption generation. The whole pipeline runs client-side in the browser.

## Setup Workflow

### Step 1: Open Cardboard in Your Browser

Navigate to [https://www.usecardboard.com](https://www.usecardboard.com) and sign in with your email or Google account. No desktop app needed — everything runs in the browser tab.

### Step 2: Upload Your Footage

Drag and drop video files or import from a URL. Cardboard supports common formats (MP4, MOV, WebM). Free tier has storage limits; paid plans offer more.

### Step 3: Describe Your Edit

Instead of a timeline, you write what you want:

```text
Cut the intro, keep the talking head segments, add captions,
and make the product demo clip full-screen
```

The AI agent interprets this and executes the edit autonomously.

### Step 4: Review and Export

The agent presents the edited result. You can request adjustments in natural language, then export as MP4.

## Deeper Analysis

### Strengths

- **Zero install** — full editor in a browser tab, works on any OS
- **Natural language editing** — no timeline, no keyframes, just describe
- **YC backing** — Y Combinator W26 batch adds credibility
- **Caption generation** — automatic captions without a separate ASR step

### Limitations

- **Processing latency** — AI scene analysis runs on remote infrastructure, not locally. Complex edits take minutes.
- **Storage caps** — free tier is limited; power users hit limits fast
- **Limited fine control** — if you need precise frame-level edits, you'll hit walls
- **Output quality** — AI-composed cuts are good, not broadcast-ready

### Use Cases

- Social media content (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) where speed matters more than perfection
- Quick demo videos for internal use
- Content creators who want to ship fast without learning a full NLE

## FAQ

**Q: Is Cardboard free to use?**
**A:** Cardboard has a free tier with limited storage and exports. Paid plans unlock higher usage.

**Q: Can I export to professional formats?**
**A:** Exports are primarily MP4. Professional formats (ProRes, DNxHD) are not currently supported.

**Q: Does it work on mobile?**
**A:** The browser app works on tablet browsers, but the editing experience is optimized for desktop.

**Q: How does it compare to Premiere's AI features?**
