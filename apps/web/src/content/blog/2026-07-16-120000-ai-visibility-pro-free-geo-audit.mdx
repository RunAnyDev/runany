## What Is AI VISIBILITY Pro?

AI VISIBILITY Pro is a web-based GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) audit tool. Enter your domain and receive a structured report assessing how well AI systems — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Grok, and Google AI — can crawl, understand, verify, and cite your brand.

The free tier generates a locked report showing the audit structure, executive summary, and top-level findings. Paid credits unlock the full issue list, code-level fixes, schema recommendations, and a 90-day publishing roadmap.

## How It Works

### Free Audit

Enter your URL and receive a locked AI Visibility report that covers:

- **Full-site crawl inventory** — audits homepage, product pages, pricing, case studies, docs, help center, blog, sitemap, robots, PDFs, and hidden entry points
- **Brand entity profile** — clarifies who you are, what you offer, product boundaries, aliases, target customers, use cases, and disambiguation risks
- **Top-level findings** — executive summary of crawlability, brand facts, evidence quality, intent coverage, technical structure, and trust signals

The free report shows the structure and selected signals so you can evaluate the tool before paying.

### Paid Credit Tiers

Credits are deducted when you unlock a generated report. The paid report includes:

- Full issue list with P0, P1, and P2 priority tiers
- Code-level fixes and content fixes
- Schema markup recommendations
- A 90-day GEO publishing roadmap

Credits are valid for one year from purchase.

## Key Features

**Full-site crawl inventory**

Audits every public surface: homepage, product pages, pricing, case studies, documentation, help center, blog, sitemap, robots.txt, PDFs, and hidden entry points. The crawl reveals which parts of your site are invisible or confusing to AI crawlers.

**Brand entity profile**

Clarifies your brand identity from an AI system's perspective — who you are, what you offer, product boundaries, aliases, target customers, use cases, regions, and disambiguation risks. Vague or inconsistent brand signals make AI citation unreliable.

**AI answer engine readiness**

The report evaluates whether AI systems can actually cite your brand for the queries that matter. It surfaces gaps in intent coverage — queries where competitors are cited but you are not.

**Priority-ranked fix roadmap**

Paid reports break issues into P0 (blocks AI understanding entirely), P1 (hurting citation confidence), and P2 (incremental improvements). Each fix is concrete: code changes, content changes, or schema additions.

**Schema recommendations**

Specific structured data suggestions to make your entities, products, and content easier for AI systems to parse and verify.

## Deeper Analysis

### Who Is This For?

The tool is designed for:

- **Shopify stores** — product pages often lack structured data and clear entity signals
- **AI SaaS companies** — technical products are poorly cited because documentation is not AI-friendly
- **B2B service firms** — broad service pages get confused with competitors; entity disambiguation is critical
- **Agencies and consultants** — delivering GEO audits as client deliverables
- **Any brand with a public website** — if you care about being cited in AI-generated answers

### What the GEO Report Does Not Do

The audit improves readiness, not ranking. The report makes your site easier for AI systems to crawl, understand, verify, and cite — but AI platforms make their own citation decisions. No audit tool can guarantee you will appear in ChatGPT answers.

### Credit Economics

Credits are consumed when you unlock a report. One report equals one credit deduction. If you re-audit the same domain after making changes, that is a new credit. Credits do not expire during the 1-year validity window.
