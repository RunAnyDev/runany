## What Is LaunchPact?

LaunchPact solves a problem every indie founder hits: you ship something great, post it on Product Hunt, and get buried because you lack early upvote momentum.

The platform works like a launch co-op. When you list your Product Hunt launch date, LaunchPact matches you with founders launching near the same time. You form **mutual pacts** — you agree to upvote each other's products on launch day, and you actually follow through.

From the LaunchPact homepage:

> "Launching on Product Hunt alone is brutal. Launch with a crew that upvotes for real. LaunchPact matches you with founders launching near your date. Form mutual pacts, show up for each other on launch day, and climb the Product Hunt rankings together."

The platform includes **verified upvotes** — screenshot + AI verification that you actually showed up. There's also a trust and reputation system built on honesty, plus smart reminders so you do not forget your pact commitments.

## How LaunchPact Works

### Step 1: List Your Launch

Go to [launchpact.io](https://www.launchpact.io) and add your upcoming Product Hunt launch. Include your product name, category, and launch date.

The first 10 pacts per launch are free.

### Step 2: Find Your Launch Crew

Browse the feed of upcoming Product Hunt launches. You can see what other founders are building, their categories, and how many days until their launch.

Back the ones you would genuinely upvote — not reciprocal box-ticking, real enthusiasm.

### Step 3: Form Mutual Pacts

When another founder backs your launch and you back theirs, a pact is formed. Both sides have to commit.

This is the accountability layer that Discord launch groups lack — it is not just a group chat, it is a bilateral commitment.

### Step 4: Show Up and Get Upvoted

On launch day, you upvote your pact partners and they upvote you. LaunchPact verifies the upvote happened via screenshot and AI check.

The result is real momentum from real users, not spam.

## Why Upvote Momentum Matters on Product Hunt

Product Hunt surfaces products partly based on early velocity. Products with 10–20 upvotes in the first hour get featured more prominently than those that trickle in over days.

For solo founders or small teams without an existing audience, getting that initial burst is the hardest part. LaunchPact turns a solo launch into a coordinated team effort.

## FAQ

**Q: Is LaunchPact free?**
**A:** Yes, the core pact and upvote system is free. The free tier includes 10 pacts per launch. Premium plans add more pacts and advanced analytics.

**Q: Does LaunchPact violate Product Hunt's rules?**
**A:** LaunchPact's upvote verification is designed to encourage genuine backing — only upvote products you would actually use and support. Product Hunt's guidelines prohibit artificial upvote inflation, so founders should only form pacts for products they would genuinely endorse.

**Q: How does upvote verification work?**
**A:** When you claim you upvoted, LaunchPact asks for a screenshot and runs AI checks to confirm the upvote appears in your history. This prevents people from claiming pacts they never honored.

**Q: Can I use LaunchPact for any type of product?**
**A:** LaunchPact is focused on Product Hunt launches. It works for SaaS, apps, hardware, and any product that can be listed on Product Hunt.
