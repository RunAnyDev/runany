## What Is Cardboard?

Cardboard is an agentic AI video editor that operates entirely inside a web browser. It was built by Cardboard Inc. and launched on Y Combinator's Winter 2026 batch (W26). The core promise is straightforward: instead of learning a video editor's UI, you describe what you want in plain language and the agent handles the execution.

From the product description on the site:

> "An agentic AI video editor in your browser. Describe your edit and Cardboard cuts, captions, reframes, and composes it. No installs, no downloads."

Key characteristics:

- **Agentic editing** — the AI reasons about your footage and executes multi-step edits (cut, caption, reframe, composite) rather than just applying a single filter
- **Fully browser-based** — no desktop app, no plugins, no download step
- **Built with Next.js and Clerk** — modern web stack; authentication handled via Clerk
- **YC W26 company** — backed by Y Combinator's Winter 2026 cohort

## Setup Workflow

Cardboard requires no local installation. The workflow is entirely cloud-first:

### Step 1: Sign Up

1. Visit [usecardboard.com](https://usecardboard.com) and click **Get started**
2. Sign up via Clerk (email, or social auth if offered)
3. No credit card required for initial access (pricing page exists but free tier may be available)

### Step 2: Import Your Footage

- Upload video files directly in the browser
- Cardboard processes clips server-side; no local rendering
- Supported formats are not explicitly documented on the landing page, but the product targets common video formats (MP4, MOV broadly)

### Step 3: Describe Your Edit

This is the core interaction. Instead of dragging timeline handles, you type a description:

```
"Cut out the pauses, add captions, and reframed to 16:9 for YouTube"
```

The agent interprets the intent and executes the edit. Based on the product description, the agent handles:

- **Cutting** — trimming silence and unwanted sections
- **Captioning** — auto-generating subtitles
- **Reframing** — adapting aspect ratio (e.g. 9:16 for short-form, 16:9 for YouTube)
- **Compositing** — layering elements, overlays, or multi-clip assembly

### Step 4: Export

- Processed video is available for download directly from the browser
- No intermediate export step or render queue visible on the landing page

## FAQ

**Q: Does Cardboard require any software install?**
**A:** No. Everything runs in the browser. No plugins, no desktop app, no download step.

**Q: Is it open source?**
**A:** No. Cardboard is a proprietary SaaS product. There is no public source repository.

**Q: What can the AI actually do to my video?**
**A:** According to the product description: cut, caption, reframe (aspect ratio change), and composite. It is agentic — it chains these operations together based on a single text description rather than requiring you to execute each step manually.

**Q: How does it compare to traditional NLEs like Premiere or DaVinci?**
**A:** Cardboard is not trying to replace frame-accurate NLE workflows. It targets creators who want to produce a finished short-form or YouTube edit without learning editing software. For precision work (color grading, VFX, multi-camera), a traditional NLE is still required.

**Q: What does it cost?**
**A:** Pricing is not displayed on the main landing page. Visit `/pricing` directly or contact the team for current rates.

**Q: What file formats does it accept?**
**A:** The landing page does not specify supported formats. Common formats like MP4 and MOV are reasonable expectations based on the product category.

## Conclusion

Cardboard represents a category shift in video editing: replacing UI interaction with natural language. Its agentic model interprets a description and executes multi-step edits — cut, caption, reframe, composite — without you touching a timeline. For creators who want fast turnaround on social or YouTube content and don't want to learn professional editing tools, this browser-based approach is worth watching.

The YC W26 backing signals investor confidence in the agentic video category. The closed-source, SaaS-only model limits self-hosting options, but for teams and individual creators who just want to describe an edit and get a video back, Cardboard's approach is clean and differentiated.
