## What Is AppLaunchFlow?

AppLaunchFlow is a browser-based tool that uses AI to automate the creation of app store marketing assets. Instead of hiring designers or manually crafting screenshots for every locale and device size, you feed it raw app screenshots and it generates localized variants, ASO-optimized copy, promotional videos, icons, and social media graphics.

The core workflow targets mobile developers, product managers, and indie app makers who need to ship across multiple app stores (iOS App Store, Google Play) and regional markets quickly.

Key capabilities:
- **Screenshot generation** — auto-resizes and annotates screenshots for all device sizes
- **ASO copy** — AI writes keyword-rich descriptions for app store listing optimization
- **Promo video clips** — turns static screenshots into short promotional videos
- **Icon and social graphics** — generates app icons and social media-ready assets
- **Translation support** — localizes assets for multiple regional markets
- **Keyword tracking** — monitors ASO keyword rankings over time

## FAQ

**Q: Does AppLaunchFlow generate the app UI from scratch?**
**A:** No. It requires source screenshots as input. It annotates, resizes, and localizes existing screenshots — it does not design the UI from a description.

**Q: What file formats does it export?**
**A:** PNG for screenshots and icons, MP4 for promo videos, JPG for social graphics. ZIP export available for bulk download.

**Q: How does it handle ASO keyword optimization?**
**A:** The AI generates description copy with keyword suggestions based on your app category and competitor analysis. You can override and refine before publishing.

**Q: Is there an API?**
**A:** At launch, AppLaunchFlow is UI-first. API access may be added in future iterations.
