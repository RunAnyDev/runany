## What Is Ozor.ai?

Ozor.ai is a web-based AI video generation platform designed for startups, marketers, and creators who need polished videos without video editing skills. You provide a prompt — or upload an existing document — and the platform generates a finished, launch-ready video.

The product targets a specific pain point: creating professional marketing and launch videos traditionally requires either expensive video production or time-consuming editing. Ozor.ai collapses that workflow into a single prompt.

Ozor.ai was shared on Hacker News as a Show HN launch.

## Setup Workflow

Ozor.ai is a fully hosted web application. There is no installation or local setup required.

### Step 1: Create a Free Account

1. Visit [ozor.ai](https://www.ozor.ai) and click **Get Started**
2. Sign up with email or a supported SSO provider
3. No credit card is required for the free tier

The free plan includes 10 video credits per month and 720p exports — enough to evaluate the product before committing to a paid plan.

### Step 2: Generate Your First Video

1. From the dashboard, click **Create Video**
2. Enter a text prompt describing the video you want (e.g., "a product launch video for a new AI-powered task manager with a modern dark theme")
3. Or upload an existing document, landing page copy, or pitch deck as the source material
4. Select a video template or let Ozor.ai auto-select based on your content
5. Click **Generate** — processing time depends on video length and queue volume

### Step 3: Download or Share

Once generation completes, you can:
- Download the video in your selected resolution (720p on Free, up to 4K on Pro/Business)
- Share the Ozor.ai-hosted link directly
- Embed the video on your site or in a pitch deck

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need video editing skills to use Ozor.ai?**
**A:** No. The core value proposition is fully automated video generation — you provide the prompt or document, and Ozor.ai handles the editing, pacing, and visual assembly.

**Q: What resolution are the exported videos?**
**A:** Free plan exports at 720p. Pro and Business plans support 4K exports.

**Q: Is there a free trial for paid features?**
**A:** The free plan exists specifically for evaluation. It includes 10 credits with 720p exports. There is no time-limited trial of the paid tiers beyond the permanent free tier.

**Q: Can I use Ozor.ai for commercial purposes?**
**A:** Yes. Generated videos from any paid plan can be used commercially. The free plan's terms should be reviewed for any usage restrictions.

**Q: Does Ozor.ai offer an API?**
**A:** No API access is documented on the current product site. For programmatic use cases, contact Ozor.ai directly or check their API documentation page.

**Q: What types of videos does Ozor.ai specialize in?**
**A:** The product is explicitly optimized for launch videos, marketing content, product announcements, social media ads, and explainer videos.

## Conclusion

Ozor.ai targets a specific and real problem: turning written ideas into polished video content without requiring video production skills or a large budget. The document-to-video workflow is its most differentiated feature, letting teams repurpose existing landing pages and pitch decks directly into video format.

The free tier is genuinely useful for evaluation — 10 credits at 720p gives you enough to test real prompts without any financial commitment. If the output quality meets your bar for actual product launches, the Pro plan at $19/month becomes cost-competitive with even a single freelance video editor session.

The main trade-off is control: fully automated generation means no fine-tuning of cuts, pacing, or branding beyond what the AI selects. For teams that need pixel-perfect marketing assets, this may not replace a video professional — but for indie makers and early-stage startups who need to ship launch videos fast, Ozor.ai fills a practical gap.
