## What Is Bloomiro?

Bloomiro is a search visibility action platform built for SaaS teams. It combines traditional SEO monitoring (Google Search Console) with generative engine optimization (GEO) — tracking how your site appears in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.

The core workflow: connect your site, run a scan, and get a ranked list of fixes — from missing schema markup and content gaps to competitor weaknesses — delivered as tasks you can hand off to an AI agent via the MCP protocol.

## Features

### Search Console Integration
Connects directly to your Google Search Console property. Pulls existing query data, click-through rates, and ranking positions without manual export.

### AI Answer Monitoring
Tracks whether your pages appear in AI-generated answers across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. Not just ranking position — actual inclusion in model responses.

### Site and Page Scanning
- **Site Scan:** full-domain audit for technical SEO issues, schema gaps, and content structure
- **Page Scan:** per-URL analysis for live pages

### Competitor Tracking
Scans competitor domains alongside your own, flagging where others rank for terms you do not and identifying content gaps.

### MCP Integration
Bloomiro exposes an MCP server endpoint. You can connect it to:

- **Cursor** — ask Bloomiro about your site's issues inside the IDE
- **Claude Desktop** — query your SEO data as part of a Claude conversation
- **ChatGPT** — pull visibility tasks into a ChatGPT workflow

## How It Works

### Step 1: Connect Your Site

Sign up at [bloomiro.com](https://bloomiro.com) and connect your Google Search Console property. Bloomiro reads your existing search data — no new tracking code required.

### Step 2: Run a Scan

Trigger a Site Scan or Page Scan from the dashboard. Bloomiro audits technical structure, schema markup, content quality, and backlink signals.

### Step 3: Ask Bloomiro via MCP

With the MCP server configured, you can ask natural-language questions about your visibility:

```
What pages are losing rankings in Google?
Which competitors outrank us for our top queries?
What schema markup is missing from our pricing page?
```

Bloomiro returns structured tasks prioritized by potential impact, not just raw data dumps.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Bloomiro work with sites not on Google Search Console?**
**A:** Partial. Site Scan and Page Scan work without GSC, but AI answer monitoring and query-level data require an active GSC connection.

**Q: Is the MCP server self-hosted or cloud?**
**A:** Cloud-hosted. You configure the MCP endpoint in your AI client (Cursor/Claude Desktop) and authenticate with your Bloomiro account.

**Q: Which AI models does the MCP integration support?**
**A:** Any AI client that supports the MCP protocol — confirmed working with Cursor, Claude Desktop, and ChatGPT.

**Q: Does Bloomiro write content for you?**
**A:** No. Bloomiro identifies what needs to be fixed and prioritizes tasks. It does not generate or rewrite content directly.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
**A:** Not specified on the pricing page. Starter plan at $29/month is the entry point.
