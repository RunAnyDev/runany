## What Is Podsee?

Podsee is an AI-powered podcast companion that transforms episodes into structured, actionable content. Rather than passively listening, you can actively engage with podcast material through transcripts, summaries, mind maps and interactive chat. The product targets podcast learners, researchers, and professionals who want to extract maximum value from audio content.

Core features as described on the product page:

- **Transcripts** — detailed, searchable text for every episode
- **Summaries** — bite-sized overviews that skip the fluff
- **Mind maps** — visual diagrams of key concepts and ideas
- **AI chat** — conversational interaction with episode content as if speaking to the host
- **Outlines and notes** — structured breakdowns of episode structure

Podsee reports over 5,000 registered users who have processed more than 10,000 episodes.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Podsee work with any podcast?**
**A:** Podsee supports most publicly available podcast feeds. You paste the episode URL or RSS link and the platform processes it server-side.

**Q: Can I export the generated transcripts?**
**A:** Transcript export is available on the Basic plan and above. The free tier allows unlimited passive listening but limits AI-enhanced content.

**Q: How does the AI chat feature work?**
**A:** After processing an episode, you can ask questions in natural language and the AI references the episode content to respond. It functions as a conversational interface over the specific episode.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
**A:** Podsee is currently web-based (pods.ee). There is no dedicated mobile app mentioned on the product page.

**Q: What AI model powers Podsee?**
**A:** The product page does not specify which AI model is used. Processing happens server-side on Podsee infrastructure.

## Conclusion

Podsee fills a specific niche for podcast listeners who learn better by reading and interacting than by passive listening. The free tier is functional enough to evaluate the product — four AI episodes per month with unlimited passive listening. If you regularly wish you could search inside a podcast or take structured notes from it, Podsee is worth a try at pods.ee.
