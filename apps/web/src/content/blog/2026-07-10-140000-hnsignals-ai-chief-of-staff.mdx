## TL;DR

> **TL;DR:** HNSignals is an AI-powered intelligence tool that reads Hacker News comment threads and produces structured executive briefs — Hook, Gist, Debate, and Verdict — so you can stay current without drowning in HN threads.

## What Is HNSignals?

HNSignals is a web-based AI tool that monitors Hacker News discussions and distills them into sharp, structured briefs. Instead of reading hundreds of comments, you get a single analysis structured as four parts:

- **Hook** — the most compelling angle of the discussion
- **Gist** — the core technical or business insight
- **Debate** — the strongest counterarguments or disagreements
- **Verdict** — a clear bottom line written in the style of an elite Chief of Staff

The tool targets tech executives, startup founders, and developers who use HN as a signal source but lack time to read every thread deeply.

## How It Works

HNSignals indexes HN's comment threads via the official HN API and applies an AI analysis pipeline to generate structured reports. Each brief is written in a professional, executive voice rather than a generic summary tone.

You can search for specific topics or browse published briefs on the site. The tool covers a wide range of topics across AI, infrastructure, developer tools, and startup strategy.
