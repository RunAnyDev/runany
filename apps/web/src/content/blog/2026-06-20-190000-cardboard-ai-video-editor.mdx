## TL;DR

> **TL;DR:** Cardboard is a browser-based AI video editor from YC W26. You describe the edit you want in plain English and it handles cutting, captions, reframing, and composition — no software install needed.

## What Is Cardboard?

Cardboard is an agentic AI video editor that runs entirely in your browser. Built by Y Combinator W26 startup [usecardboard.com](https://www.usecardboard.com/), it lets you go from raw footage to a finished edited video by describing what you want in natural language — no timeline, no keyframes, no learning curve.

According to the founders on [the HN launch thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47170174), target customers are marketers, creators, and founders who need polished video content but don't want to master a traditional NLE like Premiere or DaVinci Resolve.

**Core workflow:**
1. Upload raw footage
2. Describe the edit in plain English (e.g. "cut to the best 30 seconds, add captions, reframe to 9:16")
3. Cardboard processes and returns the finished edit

## Setup Workflow

No install required — Cardboard runs in the browser.

### Step 1: Create an Account

Visit [usecardboard.com](https://www.usecardboard.com/) and sign up. The onboarding was noted as "very pleasing" by early HN commenters.

### Step 2: Upload Footage

Drag and drop your raw video files. Cardboard processes everything server-side — your footage never leaves their infrastructure during editing.

### Step 3: Describe Your Edit

Type what you want in natural language. Examples from the demo and launch thread:
- "Cut to the best 30 seconds"
- "Add captions to all speakers"
- "Reframe to 9:16 for TikTok/Reels"
- "Remove the background noise"

### Step 4: Download or Share

Cardboard outputs the edited video directly. A [demo video is available on the product site](https://www.usecardboard.com/share/fUN2i9ft8B46) showing the full workflow.
